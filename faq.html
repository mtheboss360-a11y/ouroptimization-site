Visits and treats

Meet the field team
What happens on a visit?

Our field team meets the staff on shift, introduces your business by name and leaves your approved printed materials, with permission. Each staff member gets a personal card, and every stop is logged before we leave.

What does a typical month look like?

We go back to the 5 businesses on your route every month, meet any new staff and restock your materials where the business has said yes. Your portal updates as we go. More on the monthly visits.

Who provides the printed materials?

You do: your flyers, cards or offer sheets. We supply and program the staff cards. More on drop-offs.

Who gets the treats?

Everyone on shift at each business on your route, from the front desk to the back office. Treats are shared with the whole staff on every visit, whether or not anyone refers a customer.

What are the treats?

Modest by design: coffee, pastries and snacks. Never gift cards, cash, free services or anything of real value. More about Treats for Staff.

How it works

See how it works
How do you choose the partner businesses?

Our matching software ranks the businesses near you that share your customers and don’t compete with you. You approve the list before any visit, and we skip businesses you already work with unless you ask. More on matching.

What will I see in my portal?

Every route stop by stop, the staff met at each business, where each partner stands, and every sign-up with the business and staff member it came through. Your totals show stops, cards handed out, sign-ups and bookings, so you can see what is working. More on the portal.

Do you offer anything besides the visits?

Yes, as add-ons to your referral network: Google Ads in your own account, tap-to-review tags, a website redesign and a private customer dinner.

Partners and results

Book a call
Can another business like mine get the same partners?

No. The partners we sign for you agree to promote one business of your kind, yours, for as long as you are a client, and it is written into the one-page partner agreement.

What if a route brings no sign-ups?

You see it in your portal: every business we visited and the staff we met. The relationships are yours either way.

Can’t I just walk into the business next door myself?

You can. The work is everything around it: finding the non-competing businesses that share your customers, going back every month and showing you each sign-up, while you run your business. See how it works.

Leads and your data

How the staff cards work
Who calls the leads back?

Your team does, from your portal: each sign-up shows up right away with the name, phone number and the service they asked about. The call comes from your business, never from us. Who does what.

What are the staff codes for?

Measuring your campaign: each sign-up is tagged with the business and staff member whose card it came through, so you can see which partnerships are working. Sign-ups are never used to reward anyone, and nobody is paid per referral or per customer.

Do you see my customers’ records?

No. We never receive your customer records, and partners never see who signed up. The sign-up form collects only what a new customer enters, such as a name, a phone number and the service they want.

Healthcare practices

About the partner agreement
What about the rules for healthcare practices?

Partner programs for healthcare practices are reviewed for compliance with applicable state rules before launch. Every partner business signs a one-page partner agreement for mutual promotion, nobody is paid per referral or per patient, and we’ll send the agreement to your own counsel if you’d like.

Do you see my patient records?

No. We never receive patient records, partners never see who signed up, and the sign-up form asks no health questions.

Will you make claims about my services?

No. We make no medical or health claims, and the printed materials we leave are the ones you give us.

Where can I read about my kind of practice?

On the Industries page: Med Spas & Wellness, Dental and Chiropractic & Physical Therapy, each with the kinds of nearby businesses we visit.
