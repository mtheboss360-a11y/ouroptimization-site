Referral network

About the referral network
What if I don’t have a referral network yet?

We build one with you. We use mapping software and local business data to map 15 to 20 nearby businesses whose customers could become your patients, such as gyms, salons, cafés and offices. Then we walk in, introduce your clinic to the owners and managers, and aim to sign 5 to 10 of them with a one-page partner agreement. If a partner stops taking part, we sign a new one that fits.

What does each partner get?

A partner kit: referral cards with its own code, flyers and a counter stand for its front desk. The stand and the flyers carry an NFC tag and a QR code linked to that partner. Their staff get a perk card from your clinic, the same for every employee, whether or not they ever refer anyone.

What is the code on the referral cards for?

It only tells your front desk which partner a new client came from, for people who call or walk in instead of tapping or scanning. A tap or a scan carries the partner in its link, so nobody has to type anything.

How do leads from partners reach my clinic?

A customer taps the NFC tag or scans the QR code at a partner, and a short form opens: first name, phone, an optional email, the kind of service they’re interested in and the best time to reach them. There are no health questions. They agree that your clinic can call or text them about the offer, and the form tells them a copy comes to us. The partner is built into each link, so every lead shows which partner it came from. When the form is sent, the lead goes to your front desk by email or text, with a copy to us. Your team calls or texts back to book the first visit.

What is an NFC tag?

A small chip inside a partner’s stand or flyer. Hold the top of a phone near it and the page it carries opens, with no app to download. It is the same tap people use to pay with their phone. Phones that don’t read NFC can scan the QR code printed on the same piece instead. If your front desk already has one of our tap-to-review Google stands, it uses the same kind of tag: that one opens your Google review page, and a partner’s tag opens your offer form.

Does anyone pay partners for referrals?

No. Staff at each partner get a perk from your clinic, such as a staff discount, and it is the same for every employee, whether or not they ever refer anyone. It is a perk, not a payment, and the partner agreement says no payment is made for a referral. Nobody is paid per patient.

What goes in the monthly report?

Leads from each partner, how many booked, and first-visit revenue, so you can see where your new leads come from. Your front desk gives us the bookings and revenue as totals for each partner. The numbers are for your report only, and we never drop a partner for its lead count.

Flyer network

About the flyer network
How does the flyer network work?

A representative of ours visits, in person, the businesses you choose: fourteen for each location, on two field days a month, seven businesses a day. At each visit they restock your flyers, cards or offer sheets, talk with the owner, manager or front desk, and come back to the same people the next month. The flyer network is one flat monthly fee per location, shown on our pricing page, and it never moves with referrals. How a month runs.

Who chooses the businesses?

You do. You list them on one page, for example gyms, salons, primary care offices, chiropractors, physical therapy, boutiques, country clubs and employers. You can swap any business with written notice before the next field day. If a business asks not to be visited again, it is dropped and you name a replacement.

What do I get after each field day?

A visit log, within five business days: every business visited, who we spoke with, how many flyers we left there, and any follow-up they asked for.

Can I track my flyer drops?

Yes. Every visit log says how many flyers we left at each business, so you see exactly how many you’re dropping off, and where. Month by month, you can see how many went out and which businesses got them. If you have a referral network too, each partner’s QR code and NFC tag carry its own link, so every lead from it shows which partner it came from. How tracking works.

Can I get the flyer network on its own?

Yes. It runs on its own or with the online search network. You can also add flyer drops to a dinner, which is quoted separately. If you add the online search network, the ad budget goes straight to Google from your own account. Book a 15-minute call about the flyer network.

Patient network

About the patient network
Can’t I just book a restaurant myself?

You can. The room is the easy part. The work is everything around it: invitations in your clinic’s name, the RSVP page, reminder messages, name cards and seating, the run of the evening, an offer card with its own code, the next-day follow-up and a results sheet at 30 days. We handle all of it, so you can host.

Will people actually come on a weeknight?

We don’t promise a number. Here is how the evening is built: the invitation comes from your clinic, the guests are patients who already trust you (at the Plus-One Dinner, each brings a friend they chose), guests reply on an RSVP page, and reminder messages go out before the night. We pick the date with you.

Is it a sales evening?

Yes. You give a short talk and make an offer you have approved, and guests can book on the night. We say so plainly, because that is the point of the evening.

My rep already sponsors events. Why use you?

Keep them. If your Allergan or Galderma rep sponsors events, we plan the dinner with them. What we add is the work around the evening: invitations in your clinic’s name, the RSVP page, reminder messages, seating, the run of the night, an offer card with its own code, the next-day follow-up and a results sheet at 30 days.

What does it cost?

It depends on the dinner, the number of guests and the restaurant. The restaurant and food are included. We quote it for your clinic after the 15-minute call, the price is fixed in writing before you commit, and there is no monthly contract.

Who are you?

A local marketing company in Chicago’s western suburbs. We run marketing for a wellness clinic nearby, and your flyer drops and partner stands report into your client portal. Every dinner gets a results sheet at 30 days: who came, how many were new and what they booked. You deal with us directly, from the 15-minute call on. More about us.

Working with us

How we work
How long do I sign up for?

The referral and flyer networks run month to month. Either side can end them with 30 days’ written notice, and email counts. Dinners have no monthly contract: each one is quoted on its own.

How do you talk about my clinic?

Only in the words you approve. We state no medical claim, outcome, price or guarantee beyond your approved materials, we give no medical advice, and we never discuss anyone’s treatment. You approve any offer, price or claim before it is printed or published.
