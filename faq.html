FAQ

Good questions, plain answers.

Matching, routes, personal staff codes and what your first month includes.

Matching and routes

What is Our Wellness Connections?

We are the outsourced, boots-on-the-ground growth team for independently owned med spas and wellness businesses in Chicago’s western suburbs. OUR Optimization LLC is the company behind Our Wellness Connections.

How does the business matching software work?

It scans businesses around your clinic and finds non-competing businesses that share your clientele: salons, gyms, studios, boutiques and offices. It ranks them by proximity, how established they are and fit with your clientele, then builds your partner list and routes. You approve every business before any visit.

What is a route?

One route is a rep’s visit to 5 matched businesses. The first-month trial includes 1 route; Monthly includes 2 routes a month (10 business visits); Monthly, more reach includes 4 routes a month (20 business visits). Business visits are not a promise of that many new partners each month.

What happens during a visit?

Our rep represents your clinic using your approved words and materials, introduces your staff offer, sets up personal staff NFC/QR codes, and records the visit. New partners are added as needed and approved before a visit.

Does each partner sign an agreement?

Yes. Every partner business signs a short one-page partner agreement. Nobody is paid per referral or per patient.

Staff offers and tracking

What is the staff offer?

A free treatment or sample provided by your clinic. The staff offer is the same for every staff member and is not tied to sending anyone. Every staff member can receive the same offer whether or not they ever refer anyone.

Who pays for printed materials and NFC tags?

Your clinic provides printed marketing materials and the staff offer. We program and supply NFC tags at no charge, because they are how everything is tracked.

How do personal staff codes work?

Every staff member we meet gets a personal NFC tag and QR code. A tap or scan opens your first-visit offer and identifies the staff code and partner that sent the inquiry. A tap is not itself a lead or a booking.

What does the portal show?

Routes completed, businesses visited, codes active, and leads and bookings by source. Your clinic supplies booking outcomes so they can be linked to the source. The screenshots on this website are a sample account for a fictional clinic, not client results.

Do you track people’s location?

No. We track the code and the partner, not people’s location. The sign-up form asks for consent before anyone is texted. No medical details should be entered in the form; the partner does not receive the person’s contact details.

Plans and working together

How does the first-month trial work?

Try it for one month: $250. The trial includes 1 route of 5 matched businesses near your clinic, staff introduced, NFC/QR codes set up and the client portal. The ongoing plan and fee are agreed in writing.

What do the monthly plans cost?

Monthly is $400 a month for 2 routes (10 business visits), with new partners added as needed, a client portal and monthly report. Monthly, more reach is $800 a month for 4 routes (20 business visits), a client portal and monthly report.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. The service is month to month, with 30 days’ notice to end. One flat fee never moves with referrals, leads, bookings or revenue.

Are bookings guaranteed?

No. We carry out the agreed outreach and report the response. No number of leads, bookings, reviews or amount of revenue is guaranteed. Your clinic handles eligibility, consultations and booking follow-up.

What add-ons are available?

Online Search Network (Google Ads in your own account), Review Tags and Website Design are each priced on a short call. A private patient dinner is a small optional add-on, quoted per event.
