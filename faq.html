Treats for Staff

More about Treats for Staff
What is Treats for Staff?

We bring coffee and treats to each partner business when we visit. It is simple hospitality, and it is how relationships start.

Who gets the treats?

Everyone on shift that day: the front desk, trainers, stylists, mechanics. Treats are shared with the whole staff on every visit, whether or not anyone refers a customer.

What are the treats?

Modest by design: coffee, pastries and snacks. Never gift cards, cash, free services or anything of real value.

How it works

See how it works
How do you choose the partner businesses?

We look for nearby businesses that share your customers and don’t compete with you. You approve the list before we visit anyone. How matching works.

What does a typical month look like?

We visit each business on your route, meet the staff and leave your materials with permission. We come back every month, and your portal updates as we go. Meet the field team.

What will I see in my portal?

Every stop, the staff we met and where each partner stands. Every sign-up shows there too. Take the portal tour.

Do you offer anything besides the visits?

Yes: Google Ads in your own account, tap-to-review tags, a website and a private customer dinner.

Partners and results

Book a call
What if a route brings no sign-ups?

You see that in your portal, along with every business we visited. Nothing is hidden, and the partner relationships stay yours.

Can’t I just walk into the business next door myself?

You can. The hard part is finding the right partners, going back every month and tracking each sign-up.

Can another business like mine get the same partners?

No. Your partners promote one business of your kind, yours, for as long as you are a client. See the partner agreement.

Who provides the printed materials?

You do, and we leave them at each business on your route. We program and supply the tap-and-scan tags.

Leads and your data

How sign-ups are tracked
Who calls the leads back?

Your team does, not us. Each sign-up lands in your portal with a name, phone number and the service they want.

What are the staff cards for?

Each card is tagged with the business and staff member, so you see where sign-ups come from.Sign-ups measure how your campaign is doing and are never used to reward anyone.

Do you see my customers’ records?

No. We never get your customer records, and partners never see who signed up.

Healthcare practices

About the partner agreement
What about the rules for healthcare practices?

Partner programs for healthcare practices are reviewed for compliance with applicable state rules before launch. Nobody is paid per referral or per patient, and we can send your counsel the agreement.

Do you see my patient records?

No. We never receive patient records, and the sign-up form asks no health questions.

Will you make claims about my services?

No. We make no medical or health claims, and we only use the materials you give us.

Is there a page for my kind of practice?

Yes: Med Spas & Wellness, Dental and Chiropractic & Physical Therapy. Each shows the kinds of nearby businesses we would visit.
