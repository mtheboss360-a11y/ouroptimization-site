The staff courtesy

How the courtesy works
Is giving staff a courtesy legal for a medical practice?

It is set up as a courtesy, not a payment: every staff member who receives it gets the same one, whether or not they ever send anyone, and nobody is paid per referral or per patient. That is written into a one-page partner agreement, and we send it with the courtesy terms to your medical director before you sign anything. We don’t give legal advice, so if you’d like your own counsel to review it too, we’ll send them the same pages.

What does the courtesy actually cost me?

You choose it and you cap it: a complimentary consultation, a complimentary add-on or a product sample. The trial default is a complimentary consultation, which costs you only a few minutes and often turns into a booking itself. With a cap of up to 3 staff per business, for example, a route means at most 15 courtesies.

What if the staff never send anyone?

Then the trial guarantee covers you. If your route brings in fewer than 5 tracked sign-ups from partner staff codes within 45 days, the $250 comes back, and you keep the partner relationships either way. See both guarantees.

Can’t I just walk into the gym next door myself?

You can. Walking in is the easy part. The work is everything around it: finding the non-competing businesses that share your customers (even ones you don’t know yet), giving every staff member a personal code and showing you each sign-up and who sent it, while you stay with your patients.

Can another spa get the same partners?

No. The partners we sign for you agree to promote one med spa, yours, for as long as you are a client. You can read how that is written in the sample contract.

Who pays for printing?

You provide your printed materials, and we leave them at each business on your route, with its permission. We program and supply the tap-and-scan tags at no charge.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing renews unless you choose a plan: Starter at $250 a month, Growth at $400 or Reach at $800. Plans run month to month with 30 days’ notice, for one flat fee that never moves with referrals, leads or bookings.

Leads and your data

See a sample dashboard
Who calls the leads back?

Your front desk, from the dashboard. Each sign-up shows up there right away with the name, phone, the service they asked about and who sent them, ready to call. The call comes from your clinic, not from us.

What’s in the dashboard?

Every sign-up as it arrives: name, phone, the service they asked about, and which business and staff member sent them. It also shows each business visited and when, your leads by business, and a month by month view. After every field day you also get a written visit log within 5 business days.

Do you see my patient records?

No. We never receive patient records, and the partner agreement says none are shared. The sign-up form collects only what a new client enters, such as a name, a phone number and the service they’re asking about.
