Treats for Staff

More about Treats for Staff
What is Treats for Staff?

On every visit, our field team brings coffee and treats for the entire staff of each partner business. It is simple hospitality: how relationships start, and the reason staff are glad to see us walk in.

Who gets the treats?

Everyone on shift at each partner business: the front desk, the trainers, the stylists, the mechanics, whoever is there that day. Treats are shared with the whole staff on every visit, whether or not anyone refers a customer.

What are the treats?

Modest by design: coffee, pastries and snacks. Never gift cards, cash, free services or anything of meaningful value.

Who pays for the treats?

They are included in your plan, and in the $450 trial. There is nothing extra to pay and nothing for you to buy or bring.

How it works

See how a route works
How do you choose the partner businesses?

Our business matching software maps the businesses around you and ranks the ones that share your customers and don’t compete with you. You approve the list before any visit, and we skip businesses you already work with unless you ask us to include them.

What does a typical month look like?

Our field team sets out on your route with your printed materials and brings coffee and treats for the whole staff at every stop. At each business they meet the staff, leave your materials with permission and make sure every staff member has a personal tap-to-schedule card. They log every stop before they leave, your portal updates as they go, and they come back the next month. Your plan sets the number of stops: [STOPS PER MONTH: NEIGHBORHOOD] a month on Neighborhood, [STOPS PER MONTH: COMMUNITY] a month on Community.

How long does a campaign run?

A campaign runs [CAMPAIGN LENGTH]. It starts with your first-month trial, and after that the field team goes back to your partners every month for as long as you are on a plan.

Is there a minimum commitment?

The trial is one month, and nothing renews unless you choose a plan. The minimum commitment on a plan is [MINIMUM TERM]. Plans run month to month, with 30 days’ notice.

What will I see in my portal?

Everything the field team does for you: every route, stop by stop, with the staff they met at each business, and every partner business and where it stands: visited, interested, an active partner, or due a follow-up. Every sign-up shows there too. More on leads and your data.

Routes and the trial

See pricing
What do I get for the $450 trial?

One route for your whole first month. You approve the matched businesses, and our field team visits each one, meets the staff, leaves your printed materials with permission and gives every staff member a personal tap-to-schedule card. If we haven’t visited all 5 businesses and set up your codes within 30 days, the $450 comes back. See the trial guarantee.

What if a route brings no sign-ups?

Then you see that in your portal, along with every business we visited and every staff member we met, so nothing is hidden. The trial is one month, nothing renews unless you choose a plan, and the partner relationships are yours to keep.

Can’t I just walk into the business next door myself?

You can. Walking in is the easy part. The work is everything around it: finding the non-competing businesses that share your customers (even ones you don’t know yet), going back every month, giving every staff member a personal tap-to-schedule card and showing you each sign-up and the code it came through, while you run your business.

Can another business like mine get the same partners?

No. The partners we sign for you agree to promote one business of your kind, yours, for as long as you are a client. It is written into the one-page partner agreement, and you see it before you sign anything.

Who pays for printing?

You provide your printed materials, and we leave them at each business on your route, with its permission. We program and supply the tap-and-scan tags at no charge.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing renews unless you choose a plan: Neighborhood at $600 a month for 1 route, or Community at $1,100 a month for 2 routes. After a minimum term of [MINIMUM TERM], plans run month to month with 30 days’ notice, for one flat fee that never moves with referrals, leads or bookings.

Leads and your data

See the sample portal
Who calls the leads back?

Your team, from your portal. Each sign-up shows up there right away with the name, phone number and the service they asked about, ready to call. The call comes from your business, not from us.

What’s in the portal?

Every sign-up as it arrives: name, phone, the service they asked about, and the business and staff member whose code it came through, so your team can call them. Your totals show stops visited, referral cards handed out, sign-ups and bookings, so you can see which businesses, visits and routes are working.

What are the staff codes for?

Measuring your campaign. Each staff member’s card and code is tagged with their business and their name, so every sign-up shows where it came through. That is how you see which partnerships are working. Sign-ups measure how your campaign is performing; they are never a basis for rewarding, thanking or paying anyone, and nobody is paid per referral or per customer.

Do you see my customers’ records?

No. We never receive your customer records, and partners never see who signed up. The sign-up form collects only what a new customer enters, such as a name, a phone number and the service they’re asking about.

Healthcare practices

About the partner agreement
What about the rules for healthcare practices?

Partner programs for healthcare practices are reviewed for compliance with applicable state rules before launch. Every partner business signs a one-page partner agreement: mutual promotion, and nobody is paid per referral or per patient. If you’d like your own counsel to review the partner agreement, we’ll send it to them.

Do you see my patient records?

No. We never receive patient records, and partners never see who signed up. The sign-up form asks no health questions, and your front desk makes the calls.

Will you make claims about my services?

No. We make no medical or health claims and never describe your services in words you haven’t approved. The printed materials we leave are the ones you give us.

Is there a page for my kind of practice?

Yes: Med Spas & Wellness, Dental and Chiropractic & Physical Therapy, each with the kinds of businesses we would look at near a practice like yours.
