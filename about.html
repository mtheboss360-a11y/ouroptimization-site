How we work

What we put in writing.

These are in our written agreements.

  • Your words only

    We never describe your business or your services in words you haven’t approved. For healthcare practices, we make no medical or health claims.

  • No customer records

    We never receive your customer records, and partners never see who signed up. The sign-up form collects only what a new customer enters. For healthcare practices: we never receive patient records, and the sign-up form asks no health questions.

  • What stays yours

    Your lists, photos and materials stay yours, and we use them only for the work you hired us for. You keep your partner relationships.

  • A one-page partner agreement

    Every partner business signs one, and you see it before you sign anything.
