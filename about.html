How we work

Six things we put in writing.

These are in our written agreements.

  • Your words only

    We talk about your clinic only in the words you approve. No medical claim, outcome, price or guarantee beyond your approved materials, and no medical advice.

  • A log of every visit

    Within five business days of each field day, you get a visit log: every business visited, who was spoken to, what was left and any follow-up asked for.

  • Yours to keep

    Your lists, materials, photos, content and logos belong to your clinic, while we work together and after.

  • Fees set in advance

    Each dinner has one fixed quote. Monthly services have a flat monthly fee. Neither is based on the number or value of patients anyone sends you.

  • Independent

    We work for your clinic as an independent company. We never pay anyone for referrals, or offer anything of value in exchange for sending patients.

  • Month to month

    The referral network routes run month to month. Either side can end them with 30 days’ written notice, and email counts. Dinners have no monthly contract.
