How we work

Six things we put in writing.

These are in our written agreements.

  • Your words only

    We never describe your treatments in words you haven’t approved, and we make no medical claims.

  • One flat fee

    Your fee is set in advance and never moves with referrals, leads or bookings.

  • Month to month

    After the trial, nothing renews unless you choose a plan. Plans run month to month, with 30 days’ notice.

  • A one-page partner agreement

    Every partner business signs one: mutual promotion, and nobody is paid per referral or per patient. You see it and the courtesy terms before you sign anything.

  • No patient records

    We never receive patient records, and none are shared with partners. The sign-up form collects only what a new client enters.

  • Two trial guarantees

    If we haven’t visited all 5 businesses and set up your codes within 30 days, or your route brings in fewer than 5 tracked sign-ups from partner staff codes within 45 days, the $450 comes back.
