Our story

It started with my mom’s clinic.

I’m Mustafa Siddiqui. My mom, Dr. Farhana, runs a pediatric clinic. I started out as her marketing team, and I ran the flyers for her business myself.

While I did, her clinic grew from 380K in revenue to more than 800K. I got to see what that meant for her.

That is why I started this network. Every local business deserves someone who shows up for it the way I showed up for my mom. When you work with us, you work with me directly.
