Owner portal

Everything we do for you, in one place.

For you, the owner. See what is happening next, which businesses were visited and who asked for a call.

What you see

  • What happens next, and who is doing it.
  • The businesses near you, and where each one stands.
  • Every visit, with its notes and the materials left.
  • Every callback request, with the name, how to reach them and the partner who sent it.
  • A campaign report: link opens, requests and what your team did with each one.

What you do

  • Pick up to five businesses for a route. We put the route together.
  • Download your QR code, print your referral card and copy the tap link for each partner.
  • Work the callback inbox yourself, any time.

Front desk portal

Every request, ready to call.

For your front desk. One inbox for callback requests, and one list for the follow-ups each visit leaves.

What they see

  • Each request: the name, how they want to be reached, and the partner who sent them.
  • The follow-up each visit left, with the runner’s note and the date it is due.

What they do

  • Tap to call, add a note, and mark the request Contacted, Scheduled or Closed.
  • Mark each follow-up To do, Needs a follow-up or Done.

What this login never opens

  • The routes, the map or the campaign report.
  • A way to export the contact list. There is no download or copy-all button.

The portal does not book appointments. Scheduled means your team confirmed it.

Runner portal

The route, stop by stop.

For whoever walks the route: our field team, or your own staff when your team visits.

What they see

  • Their week, with each day’s stops in order.
  • At each stop: who it is, why we are visiting and what to say.
  • What they are carrying: flyers and staff cards.

What they do

  • Reorder the stops. The times update.
  • Pull up the stop’s QR code, printable card and tap link.
  • Record the visit: how it went, what they left, notes and the next step.

What this login never opens

  • Callback requests, or the names and numbers in them.
  • Another runner’s routes.

Who sees what

Each login sees only its own job.
