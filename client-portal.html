What each network reports

One portal, every network.

The networks run in different places: a partner’s front desk, a stack of flyers, a tag on a counter. They all report into the same screen, so you are not holding three stories in your head.

  • Referral network

    Every partner has their own link and their own tag, so each one is counted separately.

  • Flyer network

    The visit log: which businesses we walked into, how many flyers we left, on which field day.

  • Review tags

    Each stand is its own tag, so a tap at the front desk is not confused with one in the treatment room.

  • All of it together

    One place to look, rather than a spreadsheet for each thing we do.

Month by month

The whole program, a row at a time.

Every month of the work is one row: the field days we worked, the flyers that went out, and the people who came in. Pick a month and the rest of the report follows it, so you can compare a slow month with a good one instead of remembering them.

  • By business. Which of your partner businesses people are actually coming from, ranked, so you know where to spend the next field day.
  • By drop. Each visit in order, with the date and the flyers counted, the same log the representative fills in on the day.
  • The list itself. Everyone who came in, which business sent them and when, so your front desk can pick up the phone.

Your login

One login, your clinic only.

You get a login when your first field day is booked. It opens your clinic’s own report and nothing else: no other clinic’s records are reachable from it, and there is no shared account.

  • Yours when we start

    We set it up with the first field day and send the details to the person you name.

  • Live, not a monthly PDF

    The report updates as the work happens. You do not wait for a summary at the end of the month.

  • Share it inside your clinic

    Tell us who else should see it and we will set them up.

  • Yours to keep reading

    The report stays open to you for as long as we are working together.
