Add-on · Tap-to-review tags

One tap to your Google review page.

A patient taps their phone on the tag, or scans its QR code, and your Google review form opens. No searching for your clinic. We make the tags in your brand, program each one with your review link and test it before it ships.

An add-on, priced on a call. The quote depends on how many tags and spots you need.

How it works

From your review link to a tag on the counter.

Tap or scan

What is an NFC tag?

A small chip inside the stand, card or sticker. Hold the top of a phone near it and the page it carries opens, with no app to download. It is the same tap people use to pay with their phone.

For phones that don’t read NFC, a QR code printed on the same piece, if it has one, opens the same page.

Your referral routes use the same tap for the staff codes, which open your first-visit offer. Review tags are separate: they open your Google review form, for patients who have already been in.

  1. 01

    We get your review link

    We take the direct link to the review form on your Google Business Profile. If the profile isn’t set up yet, we start there.

  2. 02

    We program and print

    Each NFC tag carries that link. If you want a QR code too, the same link goes into one printed on the same piece, for phones that don’t read NFC.

  3. 03

    A patient taps or scans

    They hold their phone to the tag or point the camera at the code. Your review form opens on their phone. What they write is up to them.

  4. 04

    Your team asks

    One sentence at checkout, the same for everyone: “If you have a minute, tap here and tell us how we did.” The tag does the rest.

Reviews the right way

Every patient gets the same ask.

A tag makes asking easy. The rules stay the same. These follow Google’s review policies and the FTC’s rule on consumer reviews. The tag only opens your Google review form: we never write, post or filter reviews.

  • Ask every patient

    Not only the happy ones. Everyone who visits gets the same ask, and no one is screened out first.

  • Nothing in exchange

    No discounts, gifts or prize entries for a review, whatever the review says.

  • Staff and family stay out

    People who work at your clinic, and their families, don’t review it.

  • Reply to every review

    Good or bad, your clinic replies to each review. In healthcare, a reply never confirms that someone is a patient.
