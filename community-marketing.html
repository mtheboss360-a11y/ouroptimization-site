Community touchpoints, not identity targeting

Churches, mosques, synagogues, temples and other community institutions are places where a local business may pursue an appropriate sponsorship, partnership, event or promotional placement — always through the organization’s own approval and on its terms. We do not select or exclude individual people based on race, ethnicity, religion or any other protected characteristic.

Language & localization

Speak the language of your market.

Different communities communicate differently. When appropriate, we localize your campaign’s language, messaging, offers and creative around the communication preferences of your market — English, Spanish, Arabic, Polish, Mandarin and other locally relevant languages.

  • EnglishYour Clinic

    Now welcoming new patients.

    Scan to book

    Ahora aceptamos pacientes nuevos.

    Escanee para reservar

    امسح للحجز

    Przyjmujemy nowych pacjentów.

    Zeskanuj i umów wizytę
  • 中文 · MandarinYour Clinic

    欢迎新患者预约就诊

    扫码预约

Localization, not translation alone

The offer, the imagery, the call to action and the channel can all change with the market — a bilingual mailer for one neighborhood, a Spanish-language Google Ads campaign for another, a translated flyer for a community event.

Language is a preference, not an identity

We localize because a neighborhood’s households read and search in a language — not to infer anything about who an individual is. Every translated piece is reviewed with you before it prints or goes live.
