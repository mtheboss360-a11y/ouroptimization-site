Online Search Network

Ads for the people already looking for you.

Most med spa ads chase strangers. Ours are built for the search that comes after someone hears your name, from a partner, a flyer or a dinner.

  1. 01

    Written to Google’s rules

    Med spa ads are held to Google’s health care policies, and one wrong word can get an ad turned down. We write yours to those policies from the first draft, before anything goes live.

  2. 02

    Built around your name

    Your ads are aimed at people near your clinic who search for you by name or for the treatments you offer.

  3. 03

    Your words, approved by you

    You see every ad before it runs. Nothing goes live without your OK.

  4. 04

    One clear report

    Each month you get a short report in writing: what ran, and what it cost in your Google Ads account.

Questions

Straight answers on price.

What happens after the first month?

Your referral network continues at $500 a month, month to month. Either side can end it with 30 days’ written notice, and email counts.

Does the fee go up if partners send more patients?

No. It is flat and set in advance, and it never moves with referrals, leads, bookings or revenue. Nobody is paid per patient.

Can I get the flyer network or online search on their own?

Yes. The flyer network is $700 a month per location, and online search is $200 a month plus your ad budget. Each runs on its own or with your referral network.

Who pays for the ads?

Your clinic pays Google directly, from your own Google Ads account. Our part is the flat $200 a month, and the ad budget never passes through us.

How is a dinner priced?

One flat fee per event, quoted per venue after the 15-minute call. It covers the restaurant, the food, one of us there all evening, the results sheet at 30 days, and everything custom we make for the night. The price is fixed in writing before you commit, and there is no monthly contract.
