Pricing

    $600 a month

A route is 5 matched businesses near you, visited in person by our team. Plans start after your first month.

Every plan includes

  • In-person visits
  • Approved materials, left at each business (you supply the printing)
  • Trackable staff cards, supplied at no charge
  • Modest treats for each partner’s staff on every visit: coffee, pastries and snacks

The terms

  • Nothing renews on its own after the first month.
  • After any minimum term agreed in writing, plans run month to month, with 30 days’ notice.
  • One flat fee. It never changes with referrals, leads or bookings, and nobody is paid per referral or per customer.
