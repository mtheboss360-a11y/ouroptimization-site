Questions

Straight answers on price.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing renews on its own. To keep going, you choose Neighborhood or Community. Minimum term: [MINIMUM TERM]. Plans run month to month, with 30 days’ notice.

What is a route?

Our field team visits 5 matched businesses near you and introduces your business to their staff. You approve the list before any visit. Neighborhood is 1 route a month and Community is 2.

Does the fee go up if more people sign up?

No. It is one flat fee that never moves with referrals, leads or bookings. Nobody is paid per referral or per customer.

Who pays for the coffee and treats?

They’re included in your plan. The trial includes them too. The treats are modest by design: coffee, pastries and snacks for the whole staff. Never gift cards, cash, free services or anything of real value.

Who pays for printing?

You provide your printed materials, and we leave them at each business on your route, with its permission. We program and supply the tap-and-scan tags at no charge.

How are the add-ons priced?

On a call, once we know what your business needs. Google Ads run in your own account, so the ad budget goes to Google, not to us.
