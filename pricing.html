Questions

Straight answers on price.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing renews on its own. To keep going, you choose Neighborhood or Community. Plans run month to month, with 30 days’ notice.

What is a route?

A rep visits 5 matched businesses near your clinic and introduces your clinic to their staff. You approve the list before any visit. Neighborhood is 1 route a month and Community is 2.

Does the fee go up if partners send more patients?

No. It is one flat fee that never moves with referrals, leads or bookings. Nobody is paid per referral or per patient.

What does the courtesy cost me?

You choose it, and every staff member at a partner business gets the same one. The trial default is a complimentary consultation, which costs you only a few minutes and often turns into a booking itself.

Who pays for printing?

You provide your printed materials, and we leave them at each business on your route, with its permission. We program and supply the tap-and-scan tags at no charge.

How are the add-ons priced?

On a call, once we know what your clinic needs. Google Ads run in your own account, so the ad budget goes to Google, not to us.
