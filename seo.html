What we work on

Four parts of local search a business controls.

  • Google Business Profile

    The listing people see in Google Maps and local results. We set it up, or take over the one you have, and optimize it: categories, services, hours, photos, booking link and regular updates.

    When the campaign offer or a service changes, the profile changes with it.

  • Local keywords and pages

    Local keyword and on-page updates to your website, with a page for each core service and the area you serve, written around what people nearby search for.

    Each page carries the campaign offer and a tracked way to call or book, with a translated version where the market searches in another language.

  • Consistent listings

    Your business name, address, phone number and hours, matched across Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and the directories your industry uses.

    We find every mismatched detail and fix it, so customers and search engines see one set of facts.

  • Reviews from real customers

    A review-request system that asks every customer for a review after their visit, and a reply to each review. Healthcare replies never confirm that someone is a patient.

    We never write, buy or filter reviews, and never offer anything in exchange for one.

How it runs

From an audit to a routine your team can keep.

  1. AuditProfile, listings, pages and reviews, checked against the market study.
  2. FixWrong or missing details corrected everywhere they appear.
  3. BuildLocal pages for your core services and the area you serve.
  4. Ask and replyA review routine short enough for the front desk to keep.
  5. ReportA monthly report and strategy call, with search next to the rest of the campaign.

What to expect

Measured honestly. No promised rankings.

What we report

Calls, direction requests and website clicks from your Business Profile, visits to the local pages, and offer codes redeemed from them.

Search is reported next to mail, flyers, events and ads, so you can compare channels.

What no one can promise

Google decides what appears in local results. It weighs relevance, distance and prominence, and how far a searcher is from you is outside anyone’s control.

We don’t promise rankings or positions. We control the work: an accurate, complete profile, consistent listings, local pages and a steady review routine.
