Pillar 7 · Offers & Promotions

Promotional platforms: Groupon and deal sites.

A deal site puts an introductory offer in front of people browsing for services nearby. It is an optional tool. We use it for a specific goal, plan it with the rest of your campaign and go through the trade-offs with you first.

Set Up a Strategy MeetingSee How It Works

When it fits

An optional tool, for specific goals.

A campaign doesn’t need a deal site. One is worth a look when a clear goal could use it, and worth skipping when there isn’t one.

It fits when you are

  • Filling new capacity, such as a new provider, a new room or longer hours
  • Introducing a new service to people who haven’t tried it
  • Adding bookings on quieter days or in a slower season
  • Reaching people who browse deal sites and may not see your other channels

It usually doesn’t fit when

  • Your schedule is already full
  • The service can’t carry a deep discount and still work for you
  • Your brand depends on never being discounted

The trade-offs

What a deal costs, before you decide.

We go through these with you in a strategy meeting, using your own prices and schedule, before recommending a deal.

  • Discount depth

    A deal usually needs a visible discount to stand out. The price has to work for you even at that depth.

  • The platform’s commission

    Groupon charges its own commission on redeemed vouchers, separate from our fee. The terms are set in your agreement with Groupon.

  • Buyers who may not return

    Some buyers come for the discount and move on to the next deal. A first visit is not yet a regular customer.

  • Capacity

    A popular deal can sell more vouchers than your schedule can absorb, and redemptions can bunch up before they expire.

We don’t promise sales, sell-outs or repeat visits from a deal.

How we structure the offer

An offer built to lead to a second visit.

The terms do most of the work of keeping a deal under control. You approve every one before anything is published.

  • A service that leads naturally to a follow-up visit or a package
  • New customers only, one per person
  • Purchase limits sized to the capacity you actually have
  • Booking required, with expiry terms that spread visits out
  • Listing copy and photos that match the rest of the campaign

Groupon promotion management is part of the Digital Growth Package ($900/month): deal structure, listing copy and images, ongoing management and redemption tracking.

Ad spend and platform fees are separate and paid directly to Meta and Groupon.

The follow-up

Turning a first visit into a repeat customer.

The deal brings someone in once. What happens at that visit, and after it, decides whether they come back.

  1. BookingA confirmation with directions and what to expect.
  2. First visitA welcome kit to take home, with your name and number on it.
  3. Before they leaveA reason to book the next visit, such as a return offer or a package.
  4. AfterwardWith their permission, a thank-you with a return offer and its own code.
  5. TrackedRedemptions and rebookings counted against the deal.

Welcome kits carry the same branding as the rest of your campaign, and return offers use the same gift-card format as your mailers.

Part of one campaign.

A deal listing runs on the same offer plan and tracking as your mail, flyers and ads.
