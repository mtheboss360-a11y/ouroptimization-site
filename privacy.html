Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This website is operated by OUR Optimization LLC, trading as Our Wellness Connections. We represent independent med spas and wellness businesses through local matching and referral routes. This policy covers our contact form, the NFC/QR first-visit sign-up form, event replies and the Baby & Kids Health Expo forms described below. It explains what we collect, how it is used and how to contact us about your information.

Who we are

OUR Optimization LLC (trading as Our Wellness Connections) is responsible for the information described in this policy. Our website is https://ouroptimization.com.

You can reach us about privacy by email at mustafa@ouroptimization.com.

What the contact form collects

The Book a 15-minute call form collects your name, clinic name, phone number, optional email, town, best time to call, service selection and optional message. The service choices are Referral network (routes), Online search / review tags / website, Patient dinner and Not sure yet. We use this information to respond to your request and follow up about that request, not to add you to unrelated marketing lists. Please don’t include any patient information.

Guests who reply to an invitation

We set up the RSVP page for the dinner, so we see the replies guests send there. We use them for the reminder messages, the name cards and seating, the next-day follow-up and your clinic’s results sheet.

The rest of this policy applies to those replies too. We do not sell them or pass them to anyone for their own marketing, and a guest can ask us what we hold about them, or ask us to delete it, as described below.

People who use an NFC/QR sign-up form

Every staff member we meet receives a personal NFC tag and QR code. A tap or scan opens the clinic’s first-visit offer and carries a source code identifying the staff code and partner business. We track the code and the partner, not people’s location; we do not request GPS access for this tracking.

The form asks for first name, phone number, optional email, service interest and the best time to reach you. It asks for no medical history. Please do not include patient information or health details. A tap or scan is not itself a submitted inquiry or a booking.

The sign-up form asks for consent before anyone is texted and explains that your submission goes to the clinic with a copy to us. A staff member’s code does not give that person or the partner access to your contact details. The clinic uses the information to follow up about the offer and handles it under its own privacy practices. You may ask the clinic to stop contacting you.

We use source codes to report inquiries and bookings by source. Booking outcomes come from the clinic; a scan alone does not establish a booking. We also keep partner contact details and the association between personal staff codes and their partner so we can operate the network. We do not sell these details.

Earlier forms on this site

This site used to have a Strategy Meeting request form. It has been retired. Any submission we still hold from it, or from any earlier form on this site, is handled the same way as a call request under this policy: it is used only for what you asked us about, it is held as described below, and we delete it when you ask.

How we use it

We use what you send to call you back at the time you chose, to talk through what fits your clinic (the referral network routes, a dinner, the online search network, a website redesign or review tags), and to follow up with you about it by phone or email.

If you decide to go ahead with a dinner, we use the same information to plan and run it with you: the restaurant, the menu, the invitations in your clinic’s name, the RSVP page, the follow-up and the results sheet. If you go ahead with the referral network routes or the online search network, with or without a dinner, we use it for those as well.

Filling in the call request form does not put you on any unrelated marketing list.

How it is sent and where it is held

This is a static site hosted on Netlify. Your submission is sent over HTTPS, so it is encrypted in transit between your browser and the site.

Submissions from the call request form and the expo table form are handled by Netlify Forms and emailed to us. So your inquiry reaches Netlify, as our hosting and form provider, and our own email inbox, where we read it and reply.

Partner offer form submissions are also sent over HTTPS, and are delivered to the clinic’s front desk, and to us, through email and text services.

We do not sell or rent your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not pass it to data brokers or list sellers. Your details are never passed to anyone for their own marketing unless you tick a box asking us to, as described for the partner offer form above and the expo below.

Who else sees it

We share your information only with the service providers we need in order to deliver the work you hire us for, and only the part of it they need to do their job. Where part of that work is carried out by a supplier, such as the restaurant hosting your dinner or a printer, that supplier is given only what it needs for that job, not your whole inquiry.

If your clinic’s Allergan or Galderma rep sponsors events, we plan the dinner with them. They too are given only the part of your information they need for that.

Netlify sees form submissions as part of hosting this site and handling the forms, as described above.

When you claim an offer at a partner, the clinic making the offer receives what you send, as described above. The email and text services that deliver it carry it to the clinic’s front desk and to us.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to.

Baby & Kids Health Expo

Practices. The table request form at /expo-vendors collects your practice name, specialty, your name and role, email, phone, website or Instagram, any optional extras you tick (a logo tablecloth or custom gifts) and any notes. We use it to organize the expo and to contact you about your table.

Families. Each provider’s table has its own QR code that opens a short form for that provider only. What you enter there goes to that provider, and only if you agree to it on the form. No other provider receives it from us. Each provider then handles your details under its own privacy practices.

To have a request or a table request deleted, use the address below.

How we do not use your information

We do not use the information you send us to build an advertising profile of you.

Asking what we hold, or asking us to delete it

Email mustafa@ouroptimization.com and tell us what you want: a copy of what we hold about you, a correction to it, or deletion. Where you can, write from the email address you used on the form, or tell us the phone number you gave, so we can find your submission.

We will delete your submission from our records when you ask. If your details have already gone to a supplier for a dinner that is already booked, such as the restaurant or a printer, we will tell you what has been sent and what we can still pull back.

Contacting us about privacy

Email: mustafa@ouroptimization.com
