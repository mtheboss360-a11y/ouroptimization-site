Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This website is operated by OUR Optimization LLC (trading as Our Wellness Optimization). We are a local marketing company that builds referral networks, runs flyer drops and plans private patient dinners for independent med spas. The only information we ask for on this site is what we need to call you back about your request, and the practice details on our Baby & Kids Health Expo table form. When we run a referral network for a clinic, we also run a short form for that clinic’s first-visit offer. That form is separate from this site, and is described below. This page explains what each form collects, where that information goes, who else can see it, and how to ask us to delete it. Questions about any of it: mustafa@ouroptimization.com.

Who we are

OUR Optimization LLC (trading as Our Wellness Optimization) is responsible for the information described in this policy. Our website is https://ouroptimization.com.

You can reach us about privacy by email at mustafa@ouroptimization.com.

What the call request form collects

The “Book a 15-minute call” form on our contact page is where a clinic asks us to call about a patient dinner, flyer drops, the referral network or our other services. Apart from the expo table form described below, it is the only form on this site where we ask you to give us information about you or your clinic. The partner offer form, also described below, is separate: it opens from the tag or code at a partner. Email and the message are optional; everything else is required so we can reach you at a good time. The fields are:

  • Your name
  • Your clinic’s name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • The town your clinic is in
  • The best time to call you
  • What you would like to talk about: the referral network or flyer drops, the Plus-One Dinner, the VIP Night, Google Ads, review tags or a website, or not sure yet
  • A message (optional)

We do not ask for payment details or health information anywhere on this site.

Guests who reply to an invitation

We set up the RSVP page for the dinner, so we see the replies guests send there. We use them for the reminder messages, the name cards and seating, the next-day follow-up and your clinic’s results sheet.

The rest of this policy applies to those replies too. We do not sell them or pass them to anyone for their own marketing, and a guest can ask us what we hold about them, or ask us to delete it, as described below.

People who claim an offer at a partner

When we run a referral network for a clinic, the flyers and counter stand at each partner business carry an NFC tag and a QR code. A tap or a scan opens a short form for the clinic’s first-visit offer. Opening the form does not send us any of your details; only what you submit does. The form asks for:

  • Your first name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • The kind of service you are interested in, picked from a short list
  • The best time to reach you

Each partner has its own link, so the form also shows which partner you came from. It asks no health questions, so please don’t include any health details. You tick a box to agree that the clinic can call or text you about the offer, and the form tells you that a copy comes to us.

What you send goes to that clinic’s front desk by email or text, with a copy to us. The partner business does not receive it. The clinic contacts you about the offer and handles your details under its own privacy practices. You can tell the clinic at any time to stop calling or texting you.

We use our copy to count the leads from each partner for the clinic’s monthly report. The booking and first-visit revenue numbers in that report come from the clinic as totals for each partner. The rest of this policy applies to our copy: we do not sell it or pass it to anyone for their own marketing, and you can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, as described below.

Partner businesses. When a business signs on as a partner, we keep the name, phone number and email of the person we work with there, so we can run the partnership. If you ask, we delete them once the partnership ends.

Earlier forms on this site

This site used to have a Strategy Meeting request form. It has been retired. Any submission we still hold from it, or from any earlier form on this site, is handled the same way as a call request under this policy: it is used only for what you asked us about, it is held as described below, and we delete it when you ask.

How we use it

We use what you send to call you back at the time you chose, to talk through what fits your clinic (the referral network, flyer drops, a dinner, Google Ads, a website redesign or review tags), and to follow up with you about it by phone or email.

If you decide to go ahead with a dinner, we use the same information to plan and run it with you: the restaurant, the menu, the invitations in your clinic’s name, the RSVP page, the follow-up and the results sheet. If you go ahead with the referral network, flyer drops or Google Ads, with or without a dinner, we use it for those as well.

Filling in the call request form does not put you on any unrelated marketing list.

How it is sent and where it is held

This is a static site hosted on Netlify. Your submission is sent over HTTPS, so it is encrypted in transit between your browser and the site.

Submissions from the call request form and the expo table form are handled by Netlify Forms and emailed to us. So your inquiry reaches Netlify, as our hosting and form provider, and our own email inbox, where we read it and reply.

Partner offer form submissions are also sent over HTTPS, and are delivered to the clinic’s front desk, and to us, through email and text services.

We do not sell or rent your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not pass it to data brokers or list sellers. Your details are never passed to anyone for their own marketing unless you tick a box asking us to, as described for the partner offer form above and the expo below.

Who else sees it

We share your information only with the service providers we need in order to deliver the work you hire us for, and only the part of it they need to do their job. Where part of that work is carried out by a supplier, such as the restaurant hosting your dinner or a printer, that supplier is given only what it needs for that job, not your whole inquiry.

If your clinic’s Allergan or Galderma rep sponsors events, we plan the dinner with them. They too are given only the part of your information they need for that.

Netlify sees form submissions as part of hosting this site and handling the forms, as described above.

When you claim an offer at a partner, the clinic making the offer receives what you send, as described above. The email and text services that deliver it carry it to the clinic’s front desk and to us.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to.

Baby & Kids Health Expo

Practices. The table request form at /expo-vendors collects your practice name, specialty, your name and role, email, phone, website or Instagram, any optional extras you tick (a logo tablecloth or custom gifts) and any notes. We use it to organize the expo and to contact you about your table.

Families. Each provider’s table has its own QR code that opens a short form for that provider only. What you enter there goes to that provider, and only if you agree to it on the form. No other provider receives it from us. Each provider then handles your details under its own privacy practices.

To have a request or a table request deleted, use the address below.

How we do not use your information

We do not use the information you send us to build an advertising profile of you.

Asking what we hold, or asking us to delete it

Email mustafa@ouroptimization.com and tell us what you want: a copy of what we hold about you, a correction to it, or deletion. Where you can, write from the email address you used on the form, or tell us the phone number you gave, so we can find your submission.

We will delete your submission from our records when you ask. If your details have already gone to a supplier for a dinner that is already booked, such as the restaurant or a printer, we will tell you what has been sent and what we can still pull back.

Contacting us about privacy

Email: mustafa@ouroptimization.com
