Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This website is operated by OUR Optimization LLC, trading as Our Wellness Connections, a local company in Chicago’s western suburbs. We run referral routes for independent med spas: we introduce a clinic to the staff of the businesses near it, and show the clinic every sign-up that comes from them. We also plan private patient dinners.

On this site we ask only for what we need to call you back, a mobile number if you want our results updates by text, and the practice details on our Baby & Kids Health Expo table form. When we run referral routes for a clinic, we also run a short sign-up form for that clinic’s first-visit offer. It opens from a partner staff member’s code, it is separate from this site, and it is described below.

This page explains what each form collects, where that information goes, who else can see it, and how to ask us to delete it. Questions about any of it: mustafa@ouroptimization.com.

Who we are

OUR Optimization LLC, trading as Our Wellness Connections, is responsible for the information described in this policy. Our website is https://ouroptimization.com.

You can reach us about privacy by email at mustafa@ouroptimization.com.

What the call request form collects

The “Book a 15-minute call” form on our contact page is where a clinic asks us to call about referral routes, a patient dinner or our other services. This site has two other forms: the results updates form and the expo table form, both described below. The sign-up form, also described below, is separate: it opens from a partner staff member’s code. On the call request form, email and the message are optional; everything else is required so we can reach you at a good time. The fields are:

  • Your name
  • Your clinic’s name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • The town your clinic is in
  • The best time to call you
  • What you would like to talk about: referral routes, online search, review tags or a website, a patient dinner, or not sure yet
  • A message (optional)

The form asks you not to include any patient information, so please leave it out. We do not ask for payment details or health information anywhere on this site.

What the results updates form collects

On our home page you can leave a mobile number to get the results of our first routes by text, as they come in, good or bad. The form asks for:

  • Your mobile number
  • A tick in a box that says you agree to receive these texts from Our Wellness Connections at that number

The box is never ticked for you, and the form does not send without it.

What we text. Updates on the results of our referral routes, sent as they come in, so how often depends on how often there is news. We use your number only for these updates.

Not a condition. Agreeing to these texts is not a condition of buying anything from us, or of anything else.

Stopping. Reply STOP to any text and we stop. You can also email us and we will take your number off. Message and data rates may apply.

No marketing use. We do not sell your number, and we do not share it, or your agreement to receive texts, with anyone for their own marketing.

Guests who reply to an invitation

We set up the RSVP page for the dinner, so we see the replies guests send there. We use them for the reminder messages, the name cards and seating, the next-day follow-up and your clinic’s results sheet.

The rest of this policy applies to those replies too. We do not sell them or pass them to anyone for their own marketing, and a guest can ask us what we hold about them, or ask us to delete it, as described below.

People who claim an offer at a partner

When we run referral routes for a clinic, every staff member we meet at a partner business gets a personal tap-and-scan code: an NFC tag and a QR code. A tap or a scan opens a short sign-up form for the clinic’s first-visit offer. Opening the form does not send us any of your details; only what you submit does. The form asks for:

  • Your first name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • The kind of service you are interested in, picked from a short list
  • The best time to reach you

Each code belongs to one staff member at one business, so your sign-up is tagged with the business and the staff member who shared the code with you. You tick a box to agree that the clinic can call or text you about the offer, and the form tells you that a copy comes to us.

No health questions. The form asks no health questions, so please don’t include any health details. We never receive the clinic’s patient records.

What you send goes to the clinic making the offer, and to us, because we run the service. It appears in the clinic’s client portal, where its front desk sees it and calls you back. The partner business does not receive it. The clinic contacts you about the offer and handles your details under its own privacy practices. You can tell the clinic at any time to stop calling or texting you.

We use our copy to run the service: to show each sign-up in the clinic’s client portal, to count sign-ups by business and staff member, and to check the clinic’s trial guarantees. The rest of this policy applies to our copy: we do not sell it or pass it to anyone for their own marketing, and you can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, as described below.

Partner businesses and their staff. When a business signs on as a partner, we keep the name, phone number and email of the person we work with there, so we can run the partnership. For each staff member who gets a personal code, we keep their name and the business they work at, so each sign-up can be tagged with who shared the code. The clinic sees those names in its client portal and in our visit logs. If you ask, we delete them once the partnership ends.

Earlier forms on this site

This site used to have a Strategy Meeting request form. It has been retired. Any submission we still hold from it, or from any earlier form on this site, is handled the same way as a call request under this policy: it is used only for what you asked us about, it is held as described below, and we delete it when you ask.

How we use it

We use what you send on the call request form to call you back at the time you chose, to talk through what fits your clinic (referral routes, a patient dinner, Google Ads, review tags or a website), and to follow up with you about it by phone or email.

If you go ahead with referral routes, we use the same information to set them up and run them with you: the matched businesses you approve, the visits, the staff codes, your client portal and the visit logs. If you go ahead with a dinner, we use it to plan and run it with you: the restaurant, the menu, the invitations in your clinic’s name, the RSVP page, the follow-up and the results sheet. The same goes for any other add-on you choose.

We use the number on the results updates form only to text you results updates, as described above.

Filling in any form on this site does not put you on any unrelated marketing list.

How it is sent and where it is held

This is a static site hosted on Netlify. Your submission is sent over HTTPS, so it is encrypted in transit between your browser and the site.

Submissions from the call request form, the results updates form and the expo table form are handled by Netlify Forms and emailed to us. So what you send reaches Netlify, as our hosting and form provider, and our own email inbox, where we read it and reply.

Sign-up form submissions are also sent over HTTPS. They are delivered to the clinic’s client portal, and to us, and can also reach the clinic’s front desk through email and text services.

We do not sell or rent your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not pass it to data brokers or list sellers. Your details are never passed to anyone for their own marketing unless you tick a box asking us to, as described for the sign-up form above and the expo below. Your mobile number from the results updates form, and your agreement to receive texts, are never shared with anyone for their own marketing.

Who else sees it

We share your information only with the service providers we need in order to deliver the work you hire us for, and only the part of it they need to do their job. Where part of that work is carried out by a supplier, such as the restaurant hosting your dinner or a printer, that supplier is given only what it needs for that job, not your whole inquiry.

If your clinic’s Allergan or Galderma rep sponsors events, we plan the dinner with them. They too are given only the part of your information they need for that.

Netlify sees form submissions as part of hosting this site and handling the forms, as described above.

When you sign up through a partner staff member’s code, the clinic making the offer receives what you send, as described above. The services that host the client portal and deliver sign-ups carry it to the clinic and to us.

When we text you results updates, the phone carrier or text messaging service we send them through carries your number, only to deliver them.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to.

Baby & Kids Health Expo

Practices. The table request form at /expo-vendors collects your practice name, specialty, your name and role, email, phone, website or Instagram, any optional extras you tick (a logo tablecloth or custom gifts) and any notes. We use it to organize the expo and to contact you about your table.

Families. Each provider’s table has its own QR code that opens a short form for that provider only. What you enter there goes to that provider, and only if you agree to it on the form. No other provider receives it from us. Each provider then handles your details under its own privacy practices.

To have a request or a table request deleted, use the address below.

How we do not use your information

We do not use the information you send us to build an advertising profile of you.

Asking what we hold, or asking us to delete it

Email mustafa@ouroptimization.com and tell us what you want: a copy of what we hold about you, a correction to it, or deletion. Where you can, write from the email address you used on the form, or tell us the phone number you gave, so we can find your submission.

We will delete your submission from our records when you ask. If you signed up for a clinic’s offer, we delete our copy; the clinic keeps its own records under its own privacy practices, so you can ask the clinic as well. If your details have already gone to a supplier for a dinner that is already booked, such as the restaurant or a printer, we will tell you what has been sent and what we can still pull back.

To stop results updates, reply STOP to any text, or email us.

Contacting us about privacy

Email: mustafa@ouroptimization.com
