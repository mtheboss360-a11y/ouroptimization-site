Add-on · Website redesign

Your website, rebuilt around the patients you want.

Whether people hear about you from staff nearby, an ad or a listing, they end up on your website. We redesign it so every visitor finds the service they came for and a clear way to call or book.

An add-on, priced on a call. The quote depends on what your site needs.

What we build

Every page leads to a call or a booking.

Your services, your area and your offer decide the pages and the words. The build makes every page easy to use on a phone.

  • Mobile-first layout

    QR codes, ads and map listings open on a phone. We design every page for the phone first, then widen it for desktop.

  • A clear way to book or call

    A Book button and a tap-to-call number on every page, plus a short contact form. Each one goes to the booking tool, phone line or inbox your front desk already uses.

  • Service and area pages

    A page for each core service, and a page for each area you serve. Each page is written around what people nearby search for.

  • Copy written for your patients

    Plain words for the people you want to reach, in the languages they use. We write it with no medical claims. You approve every page.

  • Your current offer

    The offer on your printed materials and ads appears on the site with the same headline, so people find what they were promised.

  • Fast pages, accessibility basics

    Images sized for the screen and lean pages built to load fast on a phone. Readable type, strong contrast, alt text on images and forms that work with a keyboard.

  • Tracking by channel

    Calls, forms and bookings are counted by source. QR codes and landing pages tie each visit to the printed piece, ad or listing that sent it.

  • Listings that point to the right page

    Your Google Business Profile and other listings link to the page that fits: booking, a service or an area, not only the home page.

How a redesign runs

From a first call to a site you sign off on.

  1. First callYour goals, your services and how your current site works today.
  2. AuditYour current site checked page by page: phone layout, booking path, listings and tracking.
  3. Structure and copyThe page list first, then the copy for each page.
  4. DesignPhone and desktop layouts, built on your logo, colors and photos.
  5. BuildPages, forms, booking links, tracking, and redirects from your old page addresses.
  6. You approveYou review the whole site. Nothing launches until you sign off.
  7. Launch and trackWe launch, point your listings and QR codes at the new pages, and track calls, forms and bookings by channel.

What we need from you

Four things from your side.

  • Your logo, plus your brand colors and fonts if you have them.
  • Photos of your space, your team and your work. We list the shots each page needs.
  • Access to your current site, your domain and hosting, and your Google Business Profile.
  • Approvals: one person on your side who signs off on copy and design.
