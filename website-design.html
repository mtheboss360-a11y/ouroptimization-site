Add-on · Website redesign

Your website, rebuilt around the customers you want.

However people hear about you, they end up on your website. We redesign it so visitors find what they came for, and an easy way to call or book.

What we build

Every page leads to a call or a booking.

Your services, your area and your offer shape the pages and the words.

  • Built for phones first

    QR codes, ads and map listings open on a phone. So we design each page for phones first, then for computers.

  • A clear way to book or call

    A Book button and a tap-to-call number on every page, plus a short contact form. Each goes to the booking tool, phone or inbox you already use.

  • Service and area pages

    A page for each main service and each area you serve. Each one is written around what people nearby search for.

  • Words written for your customers

    Plain words, in the languages your customers use. You approve every page. We make no medical or health claims.

  • Your current offer

    The same offer and headline as your printed materials and ads, so people find what they were promised.

  • Fast and easy to use

    Light pages that load fast on a phone. Clear type, strong contrast, image descriptions for screen readers and forms that work with a keyboard.

  • Tracking by source

    We count calls, forms and bookings by where they came from: a printed piece, an ad or a listing.

  • Listings that point to the right page

    Your Google Business Profile and other listings link to the right page, not just the home page.

How a redesign runs

From a first call to a site you sign off on.

  1. First callWe talk about your goals, your services and your current site.
  2. AuditWe check your current site page by page: phone layout, booking, listings and tracking.
  3. Pages and wordsFirst the list of pages, then the words for each one.
  4. DesignLayouts for phone and computer, using your logo, colors and photos.
  5. BuildWe build the pages, forms, booking links and tracking. Old page addresses forward to the new ones.
  6. You approveYou review the whole site. Nothing launches until you sign off.
  7. Launch and trackWe launch and point your listings and QR codes to the new pages. Then we track calls, forms and bookings.

Works with the rest of your marketing.

  • Referral routes

What we need from you

Four things from your side.

  • Your logo, plus your brand colors and fonts if you have them.
  • Photos of your space, your team and your work. We list the shots each page needs.
  • Access to your current site, your domain and hosting, and your Google Business Profile.
  • One person on your side who approves the words and the design.

What a redesign covers

A redesign rebuilds the site itself.

We agree with you on which of these your site needs before we start.

What a rebuild can include

  • Service and area pages
  • Languages
  • Online booking and forms
  • Landing pages for your offers
  • Content moved from your current site
