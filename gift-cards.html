Pillar 7 · Offers & Promotions

Gift-card offers, designed to be kept.

Every campaign leads with one clear offer. Often the clearest way to present it is as a gift card: a stated value, a code and a format meant to be held on to rather than thrown away. Gift-card offers are part of Offers & Promotions, alongside promotional platforms such as Groupon.

On the mailer

The card is the focal point of the front.

On our mailers, the gift-card panel sits in the lower half of the front, under the headline offer. It is not a coupon tucked into a corner or a line of small print on the back.

Booking details sit beside it: the phone number and a QR code. The layout is designed so the eye lands on the value first, with the way to act on it right next to it.

Example Spa mailer, front

Design choices

Six choices behind the format.

Each one describes how the card is built. None of them is a promise about how many people will use it.

  1. 01

    One clear value

    A stated value tied to a named service, such as a first-visit credit or a package price, is designed to be understood in one line. “Save on treatments” is not.

  2. 02

    Wallet-card proportions

    The panel is shaped like a real gift card, with the business name, the value and a card number. It is designed to look like something worth keeping rather than another ad.

  3. 03

    A number and a code

    Each card carries its own number and code, so a redemption can be traced back to the campaign that produced it. How tracking works.

  4. 04

    Easy to carry

    “Take a picture and bring it in” is meant to move the offer onto a phone, so it is at hand when someone is ready to book.

  5. 05

    Easy to pass on

    A card can be handed to a friend or relative, which can carry the offer beyond the household that first received it.

  6. 06

    The same offer everywhere

    The card art can appear on flyers, landing pages and social ads, so the offer looks the same wherever someone meets it.

What we will not promise

We design the card to be noticed, kept and used. How an offer performs depends on the value you choose, the households it reaches and how calls are handled when they come in. We agree the value and the terms with you when we meet; we will not predict a response or redemption rate.

Offers & Promotions, beyond the card.

Some campaigns also list an offer on a promotional platform such as Groupon, to reach people already browsing local deals. Whichever way an offer runs, its codes and redemptions can be tracked.
