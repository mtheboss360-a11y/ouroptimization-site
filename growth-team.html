Growth team

Your Organic Growth Team.

The boots on the ground maintaining your relationships.

We put your community into the brand. Our people visit the businesses around your clinic in person, get to know the staff and keep those relationships going for you, with fresh-baked treats and warm coffee.

On the ground

What our people do on your route.

A route is 5 matched businesses near your clinic. You approve the list before we visit anyone.

  1. 01

    Bring treats and coffee

    Fresh-baked treats and warm coffee for the people who work there, the same for everyone, whether or not they ever send anyone.

  2. 02

    Meet the staff

    Introduce your clinic to them, using only wording you have approved, and leave your printed materials where staff and customers will see them, with the business’s permission.

  3. 03

    Set up the codes

    Each staff member we meet gets a personal tap-and-scan code, so anyone they send shows up under their name.

  4. 04

    Write it up

    After every field day you get a written visit log within 5 business days: the businesses visited, who we spoke with and what we left.

Your relationships

Maintained, in person.

  • Back on the street every month

    Your plan sets how many routes we run for you each month: 1, 2 or 4. That’s 5, 10 or 20 business visits a month.

  • You approve every business

    You approve the list before any visit, and we skip any business you already work with unless you ask.

  • The relationships are yours

    Even if a trial guarantee gives you your $250 back, you keep your partner relationships.
