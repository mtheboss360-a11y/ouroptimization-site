Our business matching software

Business matching, built for your clinic.

Our matching software scans the businesses around your clinic and finds the ones that share your customers but don’t compete with you.

Salons, gyms, studios, boutiques and offices: the same clientele, a different service. It ranks businesses by how close they are, how established they are and how well they fit your clientele, then builds your partner list and your routes.

You see the matched list and approve every business before any visit.

How it works

Your brand, out in the community.

Three steps, from the right match to a tracked booking.

  1. 01

    We match you.

    Our business matching software finds non-competing local businesses that share your customers. You approve the list before we start.

  2. 02

    We run your routes.

    A rep visits a route of 5 matched businesses and introduces your clinic to their staff with your staff offer, a free treatment or sample from your clinic. The same offer goes to every staff member, whether or not they ever send anyone. Their staff become people who have tried you and talk about you.

  3. 03

    You see every booking and where it came from.

    Every staff member we meet gets a personal NFC tag and QR code. A tap or scan opens your first-visit offer and shows who sent it. Your client portal shows routes completed, businesses visited, codes active, and leads and bookings by source.

Every business approved. Every relationship clear.

  • A partner agreement

    Every partner business signs a short one-page partner agreement. Nobody is paid per referral or per patient.

  • The same staff offer

    The staff offer is the same for every staff member and is not tied to sending anyone. You provide the free treatment or sample and approve the offer before we share it.

  • Source tracking, not location tracking

    We track the code and the partner, not people’s location. The sign-up form asks for consent before anyone is texted. Your clinic handles booking and shares booking outcomes so they can be recorded by source.
