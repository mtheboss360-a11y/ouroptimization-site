Step 1 · We show up · Matching

How we pick the businesses we visit.

We look for businesses near you that already serve your customers. Same customers, different service. You approve the list before anyone is visited.

  • Neighbors, not competitors. We never match you with a business that competes with you.
  • We skip your current partners. Any business you already work with stays off the list, unless you ask.
  • Your approved list becomes your route: 5 businesses per route.

Step 1 · We show up · Staff cards

A tap-to-schedule card for every staff member.

Each card has an NFC tag and a QR code, set up for your business. A tap or a scan opens your first-visit offer.

What is an NFC tag? A small chip, the same kind people tap to pay. Hold a phone near it and a page opens, with no app. Phones without NFC can scan the QR code.

Why we track it. Sign-ups show how your campaign is performing. They are never used to reward anyone.

  1. Tap or scanA customer taps a staff member’s card or scans its QR code.
  2. Your offer opensThey see your first-visit offer and a short sign-up form.
  3. Tagged to the cardThe sign-up is saved with the business and the staff member it came from.
  4. It lands in your portalRight away, ready for your team to call.

In writing

A one-page agreement with every partner.

When our team visits, every partner we sign for you signs it. You see it before you sign anything.

  • Mutual promotion. Each side promotes the other.
  • No pay per referral. Nobody is paid per referral or per customer.
  • Your customers stay yours. Partners never see who signed up.
  • Exclusive to you. Each partner agrees to promote one business of your kind: yours. This holds for as long as you are a client.
  • Your words only. We never describe your business in words you haven’t approved.
  • For healthcare practices: we never receive patient records. The sign-up form asks no health questions. We make no medical or health claims. More for healthcare practices.
