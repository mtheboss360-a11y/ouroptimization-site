How partner businesses are chosen.

Step 1 · We show up · Staff cards

A personal tap-to-schedule card for every staff member, programmed to your business.

Each staff member we meet gets their own tap-to-schedule card, programmed to your business: an NFC tag and a QR code. A tap opens your first-visit offer, tagged with the business and the staff member whose card it is.

What is an NFC tag? A small chip. Hold a phone near it and a page opens, with no app to download. It is the same tap people use to pay. Phones that don’t read NFC scan the QR code instead.

We program and supply the cards at no charge.

  1. Tap or scanA customer taps the staff member’s card or scans its QR code.
  2. Your offer opensYour first-visit offer, with a short sign-up form.
  3. Tagged with the codeThe business and the staff member are saved with the sign-up, so you can see which partnerships are working.
  4. It lands in your portalRight away, ready for your team to call.

Step 1 · We show up · Treats for Staff

Treats for Staff: hospitality, every visit.

On every visit, our field team brings coffee and treats for the entire staff of each partner business: the front desk, the trainers, the stylists, the mechanics, whoever is on shift. It is how relationships start, and why staff are glad to see us walk in. The treats are modest by design: coffee, pastries and snacks. They are included in your plan.

In writing

One page per partner, one business of your kind: yours.

Every partner business signs a one-page partner agreement. You see it before you sign anything.

  • Mutual promotion. Each side promotes the other.
  • Nobody is paid per referral or per customer. Our fee is flat too, and never moves with referrals, leads or bookings.
  • Your customers stay yours. Partners never see who signed up. Your team makes the calls.
  • Exclusive to you. The partners we sign for you agree to promote one business of your kind, yours, for as long as you are a client.
  • Your words only. We never describe your business in words you haven’t approved.
  • For healthcare practices: we never receive patient records; partners never see who signed up; the sign-up form asks no health questions; we make no medical or health claims and never describe your services in words you haven’t approved. More for healthcare practices.
