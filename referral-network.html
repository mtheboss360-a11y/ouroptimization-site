Step 1 · We show up · Staff cards

A personal tap-to-schedule card for every staff member, programmed to your business.

Each staff member we meet gets their own tap-to-schedule card, programmed to your business: an NFC tag and a QR code. A tap opens your first-visit offer, tagged with who sent it.

What is an NFC tag? A small chip. Hold a phone near it and a page opens, with no app to download. It is the same tap people use to pay. Phones that don’t read NFC scan the QR code instead.

We program and supply the cards at no charge.

  1. Tap or scanA customer taps the staff member’s card or scans its QR code.
  2. Your offer opensYour first-visit offer, with a short sign-up form and no health questions.
  3. Tagged with who sent itThe business and the staff member are saved with the sign-up.
  4. It lands in your dashboardRight away, ready for your front desk to call.

The staff courtesy

One courtesy, for all the staff.

Every staff member at a partner business is offered the same courtesy from your clinic, front desk, trainers and stylists alike. In the trial it is a complimentary consultation by default, and you can choose something else. It is given whether or not they ever send anyone, so it is a courtesy, not a payment for referrals.
