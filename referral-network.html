How it works

From a map of your area to a monthly report.

Five steps.

With the flyer network too, those visits happen on your field days, on the same route.

  1. 01

    Your partners

    If you already have a referral network, we start with the places on your list that send you patients. If you don’t, we build one: we map 15 to 20 nearby businesses, then walk in and aim to sign 5 to 10 of them. Each partner signs a one-page agreement with your clinic that covers display space and privacy, says no payment is made for a referral, and lets either side end it at any time.

  2. 02

    The partner kit

    Each partner gets referral cards with its own code, flyers and a counter stand for its front desk. The stand and the flyers carry an NFC tag and a QR code linked to that partner, so a customer can tap or scan. The code on the cards is for people who call or walk in instead: it only tells your front desk which partner sent them. Their staff get a perk card from your clinic, such as a staff discount, the same for every employee, whether or not they ever refer anyone. It is a perk, not a payment.

  3. 03

    Tap or scan

    A customer taps the tag or scans the code, and a short form opens for your first-visit offer. It asks no health questions, and they agree that your clinic can call or text them about the offer. When the form is sent, the lead goes to your front desk by email or text, with a copy to us, and you call or text back to book the first visit. If your front desk already has one of our tap-to-review Google stands, it is the same tap, but a partner’s tag opens your offer form instead of your review page.

  4. 04

    Every month

    Each month the flyers and cards carry your current offer, with each partner’s link on the flyers and its code on the cards. Every partner gets a planned visit each month: restocking flyers and cards, talking to staff and checking the display. If a partner stops taking part, we sign a new one that fits.

  5. 05

    Your monthly report

    At the end of each month you get a report: leads from each partner, how many booked, and first-visit revenue, so you can see where your new leads come from. Your front desk gives us the bookings and revenue as totals for each partner. The numbers are for your report only, and we never drop a partner for its lead count. Our fee is flat, set in advance, and does not change with them. We never pay a place per patient.

Tap or scan

What is an NFC tag?

A small chip inside a partner’s stand or flyer. Hold the top of a phone near it and the page it carries opens, with no app to download. It is the same tap people use to pay with their phone.

Phones that don’t read NFC can scan the QR code printed on the same piece instead. Tap and scan use different links, so you can see which one people actually use. Our tap-to-review Google stands use the same kind of tag: that one opens your Google review page, and a partner’s tag opens your offer form.
