Growth tracking and reporting

What you see, and what we review with you.

The portal shows the field work and the leads. Your growth review covers the rest of the plan, using the data your team shares with us.

In your client portal

Updated as we log the work.

  • Every drop, with the business, the date and the flyers left there
  • Leads by business and neighborhood, ranked
  • Your leads, with contact details, the service they asked about and the source
  • Month by month: materials out, leads in and the response rate
  • Past campaigns, kept for comparison

In your growth review

What we go through with you as we refine the plan.

  • Businesses contacted and relationships developed
  • Outreach completed, and the next steps with each partner
  • Referral inquiries and lead sources
  • Campaign performance, where a campaign is part of your plan
  • Appointments, conversions and attributable revenue, when your team shares that data and the result can be measured
