How a month runs

The schedule

Each location gets its own fourteen businesses and its own two field days a month, seven businesses a day, on a weekly rotation.

Four field days, every month
Field dayLocationBusinesses visitedWhen
OneLocation AIts businesses 1 to 7Week 1
TwoLocation BIts businesses 1 to 7Week 2
ThreeLocation AIts businesses 8 to 14Week 3
FourLocation BIts businesses 8 to 14Week 4

At each visit, the representative will

  • Deliver and restock the Practice’s flyers, cards or offer sheets, placed where people will see them, with the business’s permission.
  • Speak with the owner, manager or front desk, and introduce the Practice.
  • Note who was spoken to, how many flyers were left and anything the business asked for.
  • Return the next month to the same people, so the relationship builds instead of starting over.

The visit log. Within five business days of each field day, the Practice receives a visit log: every business visited, who was spoken to, how many flyers were left and any follow-up asked for. Each log is also posted to the Practice’s client portal.

What it costs

Fees

The monthly fee, by location
LocationIncluded each monthMonthly
Location A14 businesses across 2 field days$700
Location B14 businesses across 2 field days$700
TotalBoth locations$1,400 a month
  • The fee is flat and monthly. It is invoiced on the first of each month and due within 7 days.
  • The first month is invoiced on the effective date and covers the first four field days.
  • If the Practice ends the service for one location, the fee drops to $700 and the other location continues unchanged.

The fee does not move with referrals. It is a fixed amount, set in advance, and none of it goes to any business or anyone visited. Nobody is paid per patient.

The short version

In plain words.

  • Month to month

    30 days’ written notice ends it, for one location or both. Email counts.

  • You choose every business

    Fourteen for each location. Swap any of them in writing before the next field day.

  • A visit log after every field day

    Who we spoke with and any follow-up, with the flyers counted, in your client portal within five business days.

  • A flat fee

    $700 a month for each location, set in advance. It never moves with referrals.
