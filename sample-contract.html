Fees, materials and staff offer

  • The selected plan’s fee is fixed in advance. The payment schedule is recorded in the signed agreement.
  • The fee never moves with referrals, leads, bookings or revenue. Nobody is paid per referral or per patient.
  • The Practice provides printed marketing materials and its staff offer: a free treatment or sample for partner staff.
  • The staff offer is the same for every staff member and is not tied to sending anyone. It is available whether or not that staff member ever sends a patient.
  • We program and supply NFC tags at no charge, because they are how everything is tracked.
  • Google Ads, Review Tags and Website Design are separately quoted add-ons. A private patient dinner is quoted per event.

Responsibilities and signatures

Representing the clinic

We act as an independent marketing company and use only the Practice’s approved description, materials and offer. We do not provide medical advice, make medical claims or promise treatment outcomes. The Practice remains responsible for clinical eligibility and care.

Patient privacy

We track the code and the partner, not people’s location. The sign-up form requests consent before anyone is texted and asks for no medical history. Partner businesses do not receive inquiry contact details. The Practice handles follow-up and supplies booking outcomes for source reporting.

Ownership

The Practice retains ownership of its logos, copy, photographs, supplied materials and its business records. Our pre-existing matching software, tools and methods remain ours. The Practice may keep its delivered reports and approved partner list after the engagement ends.

Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other’s non-public business and personal information confidential, uses it only for the agreed work, and limits access to those who need it. This duty continues after the service ends, subject to legal disclosure requirements.

Liability and outcomes

No number of leads, bookings or amount of revenue is guaranteed. Each party is responsible for its own acts and omissions to the extent required by applicable law. Any agreed limitations of liability must be stated in the signed agreement; nothing here excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Term and approval

Either party may end the monthly service with 30 days’ written notice. The parties sign the selected plan, fee, start date and approved matched list before work begins. This is a sample for review, not an executed agreement.
