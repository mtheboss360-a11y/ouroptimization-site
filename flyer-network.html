Step 2 of 3 · Flyer Network

Boots on the ground, on set field days.

A representative of ours visits, in person, the businesses you choose. We deliver your printed materials and build a working relationship with the staff who are in a position to mention your clinic.

At a glance

  • 14 businesses for each location, and you choose every one.
  • Two field days a month for each location, seven businesses a day.
  • Every drop tracked: a visit log within five business days of each field day.
  • A flat monthly fee, month to month, with 30 days’ notice to end it.

In writing, before you sign

You see every business before you sign.

You choose the businesses, and the agreement lists every one by type and town, with the field day it is visited on, before anything starts. Two pages from a sample:

  • Read the sample contract

Tracking

Track every drop, flyer by flyer.

See exactly how many flyers you’re dropping off, and at which businesses. It all comes to you in writing, and into your client portal.

After every field day

Your visit log

Within five business days of each field day, you get a visit log. It lists every business visited, and for each one:

  • Who we spoke with
  • How many flyers we left there
  • Any follow-up they asked for

  • Month by month

    Every log counts the flyers, so month by month you can see how many went out and where.

  • Each partner, its own link

    With a referral network too, each partner’s QR code and NFC tag carry its own link, so every lead from it shows which partner it came from.

How a month runs

Fourteen businesses, two field days, every month.

Each location gets its own fourteen businesses and its own two field days each month, seven businesses per day, on a weekly rotation. This is how our flyer drop agreement runs for a two-location practice: four field days and 28 businesses a month, each one seen once a month.

One location? It gets field days one and three: its 14 businesses, seven in week 1 and seven in week 3.

Partners ride along. The fourteen are the businesses you choose for drops. Partners from your referral network, the businesses that sign on for the partner kit, sit on the same route and are visited on the same field days.

Each visit

The same people, every month.

At each visit our representative does four things, and every visit goes in your visit log.

  1. 01

    Deliver and restock

    Your flyers, cards or offer sheets are delivered and restocked, and placed where people will see them, with the location’s permission.

  2. 02

    Talk to the staff

    Our representative speaks with the owner, manager or front desk, introduces your clinic and leaves a direct line for questions.

  3. 03

    Take notes

    Who was spoken to, what was left, how your clinic was received, and anything the location asked for.

  4. 04

    Come back

    The next month, our representative returns to the same people, so the relationship builds instead of starting over.

Your list

You choose every business.

You list them on one page, anywhere you want your clinic’s name in front of people. A business can be on both locations’ lists.

  • Gyms
  • Salons
  • Primary care offices
  • Chiropractors
  • Physical therapy
  • Boutiques
  • Country clubs
  • Employers
  • Swap any business. Tell us in writing before the next field day.
  • A business can opt out. If it asks not to be visited again, it is dropped and you name a replacement.
  • Driving routes. We group the businesses into routes, so the order within a field day can change.
  • Within 25 miles. Businesses farther out can be added by agreement, with travel handled separately.

Who supplies what

Your materials, our time on the road.

You supply

  • Printed flyers, cards or offer sheets, delivered at least five days before each field day. We can design and print them for you, quoted separately.
  • Your list of businesses, and one person who approves things.
  • Your approval of any offer, price or claim before it is printed or published.

We supply

  • The representative, the vehicle, the mileage and the time for every field day.
  • The driving routes, planned around your list.
  • A visit log after every field day, with the flyers counted.

How we represent you

Your words, and nothing more.

  • Approved words only

    Our representative talks about your clinic only in the words you approve. No medical claim, outcome, price or guarantee beyond your approved materials.

  • No medical advice

    No diagnosis, and no talk about anyone’s treatment.

  • Yours to keep

    Your list, materials, photos, content and logos belong to your clinic, during and after.

  • A flat monthly fee

    Set in advance. It never moves with referrals, and no part of it goes to a business or anyone we visit. Month to month, with 30 days’ written notice to end it.
