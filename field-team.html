In person, every month.

Treats for Staff, drop-offs and every stop tracked in your portal.

Our field team does the visiting for you. They walk into the businesses on your route, meet the staff on shift and introduce your business by name.

Treats for Staff

Coffee and treats for the whole staff.

On every visit, our field team brings coffee and treats for the whole staff of each business on your route. It is simple hospitality: how relationships start, and the reason staff are glad to see us walk in.

  • Warm coffee for everyone on shift, from the counter to the back office.
  • Modest by design: fresh-baked pastries and snacks, picked up the morning of the route. Never gift cards, cash, free services or anything of real value.
  • A real introduction. We tell the staff about your business by name, using only wording you have approved.

Drop-offs

Your materials, where people will see them.

With each business’s permission, we leave your approved printed materials and give every staff member we meet a card of their own.

  • Your flyers, cards or offer sheets, placed where staff and customers will see them.
  • A personal tap-to-schedule card for each staff member, programmed to your business.
  • How the cards track sign-ups

Every month

Back on your route, stop by stop.

A route is 5 nearby businesses you approved.

  • We come back

    We go back to the businesses on your route every month. Staff who joined since our last visit get their own card.

  • We restock

    We restock your printed materials wherever the business has said yes to them.

  • We log every stop

    Before leaving each business, the field team logs who they met, what they left and where the business stands. You see it in your portal.
