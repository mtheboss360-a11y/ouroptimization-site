In person, every month.

Treats for the staff, your materials at the counter, every stop in your portal.

Every month, our field team visits businesses near you. Each one shares your customers but doesn’t compete with you. We get to know the people who work there.

Treats for Staff

Coffee and treats for the whole staff.

We bring them on every visit. It’s simple hospitality, and how relationships start.

  • Warm coffee for everyone on shift, front desk to back office.
  • Modest by design: fresh-baked pastries and snacks, picked up that morning. Never gift cards, cash, free services or anything of real value.
  • A real introduction. When staff ask who we’re with, we name your business.

Drop-offs

Your materials, where people will see them.

With each business’s permission, we leave a few things behind.

  • Your displays, made with us or by you, at the front counter.
  • A personal tap-to-schedule card for each staff member we meet, set up for your business.
  • Restocked every month. We top up your materials and cards.

At every stop

The same visit, every time.

Here is what happens when we walk in.

  1. 01

    Bring treats and coffee

    The same at every stop.

  2. 02

    Meet the staff

    We describe your business only in words you have approved.

  3. 03

    Hand out the cards

    Then we log the stop before we leave.
