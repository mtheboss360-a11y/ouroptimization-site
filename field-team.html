In person, every month.

Treats for Staff, drop-offs and every stop tracked in your portal.

Your outsourced field sales team. Every month our field team visits the businesses around you that share your customers and don’t compete with you. We get to know their staff by name and introduce your business.

Treats for Staff

Coffee and treats for the whole staff.

On every visit, our field team brings coffee and treats for the entire staff of each partner business. It is simple hospitality: how relationships start, and the reason staff are glad to see us walk in. It is included in your plan.

  • Warm coffee for everyone on shift, from the counter to the back office.
  • Modest by design: fresh-baked pastries and snacks, picked up the morning of the route. Never gift cards, cash, free services or anything of real value.
  • A real introduction. When staff ask who we represent, we name your business.

Drop-offs

Your materials, where people will see them.

With each business’s permission, we leave your printed materials at the counter and hand a personal tap-to-schedule card to every staff member we meet. Next month we restock them.

  • Your printed materials, placed where staff and customers will see them.
  • A personal tap-to-schedule card for every staff member, programmed to your business. Each code is tagged with the partner business and the staff member, so you can see which partnerships are working.
  • Restocked every month, with new staff introduced as they join.

At every stop

The same three steps, every business on your route.

A route is 5 matched businesses near you. You approve the list before we visit anyone, and the field team logs every stop before leaving it.

  1. 01

    Bring treats and coffee

    Warm coffee and fresh-baked treats for the whole staff, the same at every business on the route. Simple hospitality, included in your plan.

  2. 02

    Meet the staff

    Introduce your business to them, using only wording you have approved, and leave your printed materials where staff and customers will see them, with the business’s permission.

  3. 03

    Hand out the cards

    Each staff member we meet gets a personal tap-to-schedule card and code. Sign-ups through their code show you which businesses, visits and routes are working.

Tracking

Every stop, in your portal.

Where the field team stopped, who they met, what they left and what came back, counted from the log they fill in before leaving each stop. Every sign-up comes in with the business and staff member whose code it came through, ready for your team to call. Together the sign-ups show which businesses, visits and routes are working.

Your relationships

Maintained, in person.

  • Back on the street every month

    Your plan sets how many routes we run for you each month: 1 or 2. That’s [STOPS PER MONTH: NEIGHBORHOOD] business visits a month on Neighborhood, or [STOPS PER MONTH: COMMUNITY] on Community.

  • You approve every business

    Our business matching software ranks the businesses near you that share your customers and don’t compete with you. You approve the list before any visit, and we skip any business you already work with unless you ask.

  • The relationships are yours

    Every partner business signs a one-page partner agreement for mutual promotion with your business. Even if the trial guarantee gives you your $450 back, you keep your partner relationships.
