Add-on · Google Ads

Google Ads, for when they look you up.

Ads are there for the people who hear about you from the staff at a business near you, or from a friend, and then search for you. You pay Google directly, in your own account.

At a glance

  • Your own account. The ads run in your business’s Google Ads account.
  • Your budget goes to Google, straight from your own account.
  • An add-on to your referral network, for the people who hear about you, then search.
  • You approve the ads before they run.

Why ads come last

We drive the growth behind the ads.

People hear about your business first: from the staff at a business near you, or from a friend. Then they search for you. The ads are there for that search, so they come last.

  • Referral routes

    Our field team visits 5 matched businesses near you and introduces your business to their staff.

  • Then online search

    For the people who heard about you and search for your business.

Your account, your budget

You keep control of what you spend.

Your ads run in your business’s own Google Ads account, so you keep control of what you spend there.

  • You pay Google directly. The ad budget never passes through us.
  • Side by side with your routes. On your routes, our field team introduces your business to the staff nearby. The ads are there when people search for you.
