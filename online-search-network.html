Add-on · The cherry on top

Google Ads, for when they look you up.

Ads are there for the people who hear about you from the staff at a business near you, or from a friend, and then search for you. You pay Google directly, in your own account.

At a glance

  • Your own account. The ads run in your clinic’s Google Ads account.
  • Your budget goes to Google, straight from your own account.
  • An add-on to your referral routes, for the people who hear about you, then search.
  • Priced on a call. Our part is quoted once we know what your clinic needs.

Why ads come last

We drive the growth behind the ads.

People hear about your clinic first: from the staff at a business near you, or from a friend. Then they search for you. The ads are there for that search, so they come last, as the cherry on top.

  • Referral routes

    A rep visits 5 matched businesses near you and introduces your clinic to their staff.

  • Staff codes

    Every staff member we meet gets a personal tap-and-scan code that opens your first-visit offer.

  • Then online search

    For the people who heard about you and search for your clinic.

Your account, your budget

You keep control of what you spend.

Your ads run in your clinic’s own Google Ads account. The ad budget goes straight to Google from that account, so you keep control of what you spend there.

  • You pay Google directly. The ad budget never passes through us.
  • Side by side with your routes. The routes get your clinic recommended near you. The ads are there when those people search for you.
  • Priced on a call. Our part is quoted on a 15-minute call, separate from your ad budget.
