Who shares your customers

The businesses around you that already know your customers.

  • Clothing and menswear stores

Good to know

Made for salons and barbershops.

  • A one-page partner agreement

    Every partner business signs a one-page agreement: mutual promotion, and nobody is paid per referral or per customer.

  • No competing chairs

    Our matching software leaves other salons and barbershops off your route, and we skip any business you already work with unless you ask.

  • Your words only

    We introduce your salon or barbershop only in words you’ve approved, and we leave only the printed materials you’ve signed off on.

Questions from salon or barbershop owners

All questions
Will you visit other salons or barbershops near me?

No. Our matching software ranks the businesses near you that share your customers and don’t compete with you. You approve the list before we visit anyone.

How do sign-ups get booked?

Every sign-up shows up in your portal with the business and staff member whose code it came through. You or your front desk call and book them the way you book everyone else.

What do you leave at each business?

With each business’s permission, your printed materials where staff and customers will see them, and a personal tap-to-schedule card for every staff member we meet. We come back every month to restock and meet new staff.
