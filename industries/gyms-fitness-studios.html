Good to know

Made for gyms and studios.

  • A one-page partner agreement

    When our team visits, you and each partner agree to promote each other. Nobody is paid per referral or per customer.

  • Never another gym

    We never visit other gyms or fitness studios for you. Every stop is a business that doesn’t compete.

  • Your first-visit offer

    Each staff card opens your first-visit offer, written in your words.

Questions from gym or studio owners

All questions
What if a business on the list feels too close to mine?

Take it off the list. We only visit the businesses you approve.

Can’t I just walk into the juice bar next door myself?

You can. The hard part is finding the right businesses, visiting every month and tracking every sign-up.

Do I need to come along on the visits?

No. Our field team makes every visit, and you see each stop in your portal.
