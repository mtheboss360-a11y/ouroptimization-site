Never a competitor

Who we never connect you with.

Your partners share your clients, not your services. Here is how we keep it that way.

  • No competitors

    We never put another med spa, aesthetics or injectables practice on your route.

  • One med spa, yours

    When our team visits, each partner agrees to promote one med spa, yours, for as long as you are a client.

  • You approve every name

    You approve the list before any visit, and you can cross any business off it. We skip any business you already work with unless you ask.

What the partner agreement says

How the connection is made

In person, every month.

When our team visits, we go to every business on your route each month. Here is what happens on each visit.

Coffee and treats

For the whole staff on every visit, from the front desk to the stylists.

  • Coffee and pastries for a hair salon’s morning stylists
  • Muffins and coffee for a gym’s opening front desk crew
  • Cookies and iced coffee for a nail salon’s afternoon shift

We keep it modest: coffee, pastries and snacks. Never gift cards, cash, free services or anything of real value.

More about our visits

  • A tap-to-schedule card for each staff member

    Every staff member we meet gets a personal card with an NFC tag and a QR code. A tap or a scan opens your first-visit offer and a short sign-up form.

  • A one-page partner agreement

    Every partner signs one. Each side promotes the other. Nobody is paid per referral or per patient.

  • No patient records, ever

    We never receive patient records, and partners never see who signed up. The sign-up form asks no health questions.

  • Every visit in your portal

    Your portal logs each visit.

Your words only. We make no medical or health claims. We describe your services only in words you approve.
