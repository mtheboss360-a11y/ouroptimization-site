Baby & Kids Health Expo

An indoor afternoon where expecting and new parents meet the local providers they’ll choose for the next decade. One practice per specialty, so the families who want your kind of care find you, not your competitor.

  • Who comesExpecting parents and families with kids up to age 12
  • WhereA banquet hall near Glendale Heights, IL. Venue confirmed with your reservation.
  • FormatOne table per specialty. Your category is yours alone.
  • WhenA weekend afternoon. Date confirmed with your reservation.

What your table includes

  1. Your category, exclusively. No other practice in your specialty gets a table.
  2. Small groups stop at every table. Families rotate through the tables in small groups on a five-minute timer, so you get time to introduce yourself to every family in the room: your future patients.
  3. Your own QR code, free. Parents scan it during their five minutes at your table and leave their details for you, with their consent.
  4. Hand them a free gift. Order your custom gifts through us, made with your logo:
    • Teddy bears in a logo hoodie
    • Canvas tote bags
    • Grip socks
    • Satin eye masks
    • Insulated water bottles
    • Umbrellas
    • Polo shirts
    • Notebooks
    • Pens
  5. A list after the event of every family who asked to hear from you.
