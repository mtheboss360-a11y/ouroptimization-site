One indoor afternoon to meet the local pediatric providers your family will rely on for years: the dentist, the pediatrician, therapists, lactation support and more. One of each, all in one room, with time to ask real questions.

  • Who it’s forExpecting parents and families with kids up to age 12

How the gift bag works

  1. RSVP below. The first 150 families to RSVP have a free gift bag held for them.
  2. Check in at the door. You get your expo passport.
  3. Visit each table. Meet the provider, ask your questions and get a stamp.
  4. Hand in a full passport on your way out. You get your gift bag and an entry in the end-of-day prize draw.

Who you’ll meet

One provider per specialty, so every table is different. Categories we’re filling:

  • Pediatric dentist
  • Pediatrician
  • Speech & occupational therapy
  • Pediatric chiropractor
  • Lactation consultant
  • Doula
  • Newborn photography
  • Swim school

Gift bags are one per family, while the first 150 last, picked up in person on your way out. Gifts vary by provider.
