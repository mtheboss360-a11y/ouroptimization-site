What the service is

Our Wellness Connections is a brand of OUR Optimization LLC. We provide business matching, approved local referral routes, staff introductions, personal NFC/QR source codes and reporting for independently owned med spas and wellness businesses. A route is 5 business visits. Every partner signs a short one-page agreement. Nobody is paid per referral or per patient. The same staff offer is available to every staff member and is not tied to sending anyone.

How prices are agreed

The selected scope and fee are agreed in writing. The service is month to month, with 30 days’ notice to end. One flat fee never moves with referrals, leads, bookings or revenue.
