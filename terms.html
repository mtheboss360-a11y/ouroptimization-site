What the service is

Our service is referral routes. Depending on the plan you choose, it includes:

  • Matching. Our business matching software maps the businesses around your clinic and ranks the non-competing ones that share your customers. You approve the list before any visit, and we skip any business you already work with unless you ask.
  • Routes. On each route, a representative visits 5 matched businesses, introduces your clinic to their staff and, with each business’s permission, leaves your printed materials there.
  • Staff codes. Every staff member we meet gets a personal tap-and-scan code: an NFC tag and a QR code. A tap opens your first-visit offer and a short sign-up form, tagged with who shared it.
  • The client portal. Every sign-up appears in your client portal right away, with the name, phone, the service asked about, and which business and staff member sent it, ready for your front desk to call.
  • Visit logs. After every field day, we send you a written visit log within 5 business days.
  • The partner agreement. Every partner business signs a one-page agreement: mutual promotion, nobody is paid per referral or per patient, and no patient records are shared. We send it and the courtesy terms to your medical director before you sign anything.
  • Exclusivity. The partners we sign for you agree to promote one med spa, yours, for as long as you are a client.

The staff courtesy. You set the courtesy your clinic gives partner staff, and you cap it. For the trial, the default is a complimentary consultation. You can choose something else, like a complimentary add-on or a product sample. It is the same for every staff member and is given whether or not they ever send anyone, so it stays a courtesy and not a payment for referrals.

Add-ons. We also offer Google Ads run in your clinic’s own Google Ads account, review tags, a website and a private patient dinner. Each is agreed and quoted separately.

How prices are agreed

Published prices. Our pricing page lists our current prices: the first-month trial, and the Starter, Growth and Reach plans after it, each with the number of routes it includes each month. The prices you sign up at are confirmed in writing before you start. If we change a published price, the new price applies to new sign-ups; for a plan you already have, we tell you in writing at least 30 days before it applies.

The trial. Your first month is a trial: one route of 5 matched businesses, your codes set up and your dashboard, for the trial fee on our pricing page. After the trial, nothing renews unless you choose a plan.

The two trial guarantees. If we have not visited all 5 businesses and set up your codes within 30 days, your trial fee comes back. If your route brings in fewer than 5 tracked sign-ups from partner staff codes within 45 days, your trial fee comes back. You keep the partner relationships either way.

Plans. Each plan has one flat monthly fee, set in advance. It never moves with referrals, leads, bookings or revenue, and none of it goes to any business or anyone we visit. Nobody is paid per referral or per patient. Plans run month to month, and either side can end a plan with 30 days’ written notice; email counts.

Who supplies what. You provide your printed materials and your staff courtesy. We program and supply the tap-and-scan tags at no charge.

Add-ons. Google Ads, review tags, a website and a private patient dinner are each priced on a call and quoted for your clinic before they start. Each quote states what is included in our price and what, if anything, is paid separately. Google Ads spend is paid by your clinic directly to Google, in your own account. A dinner is one flat fee per event, with the restaurant and food included. The price is fixed in writing before you commit, and there is no monthly contract.

A quote covers the work as it was described when we quoted it. If the details change, for example a different venue or an added service, we will requote so the new price is clear to you before anything moves forward.

Treat these numbers as a directional signal about how the work is landing, not a complete or exact count of everything it produced.
