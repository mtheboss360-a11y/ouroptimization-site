Who these terms are between

“We”, “us” and “our” mean OUR Optimization LLC, operating as R Wellness Network. “You” and “your” mean the clinic, practice or business that asks us for a quote or hires us, and anyone authorized to act on its behalf.

These terms cover both the work we do for you and your use of this website. If you sign a separate written agreement or approve a written quote with us, and something in it differs from what is here, the written document governs for that work.

What the service is

We build referral networks, run flyer drops, and plan and run private patient dinners for independent med spas, and offer add-ons: the online search network, review tags and website redesigns. We are a local marketing company in Chicago’s western suburbs.

Depending on what we agree with you, the work can include:

  • The patient network, for each patient dinner (the Plus-One Dinner or the VIP Night): the restaurant and the food, the menu set with the venue, invitations designed in your clinic’s name, an RSVP page, reminder messages, name cards and seating, the run of the evening, an offer card with its own code, the next-day follow-up and a results sheet at 30 days
  • The referral network: finding businesses near your clinic, then setting up, or helping you set up, introductions and cross-promotion with them, with a one-page agreement between your clinic and each partner that says no payment is made for a referral; a partner kit for each partner (referral cards with its own code, and flyers and a counter stand carrying an NFC tag and a QR code linked to that partner); a staff perk card from your clinic; a short form for your first-visit offer that sends each lead to your front desk, with a copy to us; a planned visit for every partner each month; and a monthly report
  • The flyer network: a representative visits, in person, the businesses you choose for each location on set field days each month, delivers and restocks your printed materials, and sends you a visit log within five business days of each field day
  • The online search network: Google Ads, run in your clinic’s own Google Ads account
  • A website redesign or review tags, agreed separately

“The online search network” is our name for the Google Ads campaigns we set up and run in your clinic’s own Google Ads account. It is not a Google product. Google and Google Ads are trademarks of Google LLC, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

How prices are agreed

Published prices. Our pricing page lists our current prices: the starter pack for your first month, the flat monthly fee for the referral network after that, and the monthly fee for the flyer network (per location) and the online search network. The prices you sign up at are confirmed in writing before you start. If we change a published price, the new price applies to new sign-ups; for a monthly service you already have, we tell you in writing at least 30 days before it applies.

The patient network. Each dinner is one flat fee, quoted for your clinic per venue, with the restaurant and food included. The price is fixed in writing before you commit, and there is no monthly contract.

Everything else. Anything not priced on our pricing page, such as extra review tags or a website redesign, is quoted for your clinic before it starts. Each quote states what is included in our price and what, if anything, is paid separately. Google Ads spend is paid by your clinic directly to Google, in your own account.

Monthly services. The referral, flyer and online search networks have a flat monthly fee, set in advance (for the online search network, our fee only; your ad budget goes to Google from your own account). It does not change with referrals, leads, bookings or revenue. They run month to month, and either side can end them with 30 days’ written notice; email counts.

A quote covers the work as it was described when we quoted it. If the details change, for example a different venue or an added service, we will requote so the new price is clear to you before anything moves forward.

You review and approve the materials before they go out

Nothing goes out until you have reviewed the final materials and approved them, such as the invitations, the offer card, flyers and ads. That is your checkpoint to confirm the offer, the wording, the disclaimers, your contact details, and how each piece looks.

Your approval is the go-ahead to produce. We produce what you approved. Please read each proof carefully, because once materials are released they may already be printed, sent or published, which limits what can be changed. If you need to change or stop something, tell us as early as you can.

We do not guarantee results. Nobody honestly can.

How a dinner or any other work performs depends on the strength of your offer, who comes, your local market and competition, timing, and how well your team handles the bookings, calls and walk-ins it produces. We do not promise a number of guests, how many local businesses agree to be partners, a response rate, a number of calls, bookings, new customers or patients, revenue, or any return on what you spend. If we discuss what a dinner or an add-on might do, treat that as an expectation and a planning estimate, not a promise.

The results sheet and tracking

The results sheet, the monthly partner report, and the codes and links on offer cards, referral cards and partner stands cannot capture every response. A dinner or an add-on can produce responses it never records, for example a guest who books weeks later without the offer card, someone who searches your business name and calls, or a referral from someone who heard about you from a guest. Treat these numbers as a directional signal about how the work is landing, not a complete or exact count of everything it produced.

The bookings and first-visit revenue in the monthly partner report come from your clinic as totals for each partner.

Scope of the work

Our services are limited to what we agree with you. Each piece of work includes only what is listed in its approved quote. If something is not in the agreed scope, it is not included.

Unless we agree it in writing, our work does not include email marketing, changes to your website (a website redesign is agreed separately), or handling customer or patient calls, inquiries and scheduling. Responding to the people your dinner and your marketing reach is your team’s job, and how well that is handled matters a great deal to how the work performs.

Information you submit through this website

This site has two forms of its own. The “Book a 15-minute call” form on the contact page asks for your name, your clinic’s name, phone number, email address (optional), the town your clinic is in, the best time to call, what you would like to talk about, and a message (optional). The Baby & Kids Health Expo table request form at /expo-vendors asks for your practice details and how to reach you. When we run a referral network for your clinic, we also run a short offer form for your first-visit offer. It is separate from this site’s two forms and is described in our Privacy Policy. Leads from it reach your front desk, and us, through email and text services, so delivery depends on those services.

This is a static site hosted on Netlify. Form submissions are handled by Netlify Forms and emailed to us. What we do with what you submit is governed by our Privacy Policy.

Please do not send patient information, protected health information, or any sensitive personal data through the call or expo forms. They are business inquiry forms and are not built for that. If you need to discuss something sensitive, contact us directly instead.
