What the service is

We research local markets and create and run marketing campaigns built around them. Depending on what we agree for your campaign, that can include the work below.

We serve independently owned med spas, dental offices, pediatric offices, chiropractors, physical therapy clinics and beauty and wellness businesses, and we work in Chicago's western suburbs and the wider Chicagoland area.

  • Researching the local market, neighborhoods and community touchpoints around your business
  • Recommending the areas, channels, messaging and offer for the campaign
  • Designing campaign materials — mailers, flyers, branded materials, apparel, kits and ads — including layout and copy
  • Handling printing, production and mailing of physical materials
  • Identifying community events, sponsorships and placement opportunities, and preparing materials for them
  • Setting up and managing digital channels such as Google Ads, local SEO and Facebook and Instagram advertising
  • Configuring campaign response tracking where that applies to the campaign

Campaign pricing

We quote per campaign. The price depends on the details of that specific campaign, which normally means your market, your goals, the channels involved, the size of any print or mail run, and the area you want reached.

Each estimate states what is included in our price and what, if anything, is paid separately. Printing and postage are campaign costs; where we quote them as part of the campaign price — as we do for a direct-mail drop — there is no separate printing or postage bill added afterward.

A quote covers the campaign as it was described when we quoted it. If the details change, for example you want a larger drop, a different target area or an added channel, we will requote so the new price is clear to you before anything moves forward.

You review and approve the campaign before it goes to production

Nothing prints and nothing mails until you have reviewed the final campaign materials and approved them. That is your checkpoint to confirm the offer, the wording, the disclaimers, your contact details, and how the card looks.

Your approval is the go-ahead to produce. We produce what you approved. Please read the proof carefully, because once materials are released for production the cards may already be printing or in the mail stream, which limits what can be changed. If you need to change or stop a campaign, tell us as early as you can.

Campaign outcomes are not guaranteed

We do not guarantee results. Nobody honestly can.

How a mail campaign performs depends on the strength of your offer, the audience it reaches, your local market and competition, timing, and how well your team handles the calls and walk-ins it produces. We do not promise a response rate, a number of calls, bookings, new patients, revenue, or any return on what you spend. If we discuss what a campaign might do, treat that as an expectation and a planning estimate, not a promise.

Mail delivery timing

Once your mail is handed to the postal service, delivery is in the postal service's hands and outside our control. Weather, mail volume, holidays and local postal operations all affect when cards land.

Any in-home date or delivery window we give you is an estimate, not a guarantee. Please do not schedule a same-day event, a staffing change or an expiring offer in a way that only works if the mail lands on an exact day. Build in room.

Response tracking

Where it applies, tracking is configured per campaign, and what gets configured depends on what that campaign needs.

Tracking cannot capture every response. A campaign can produce responses it never records, for example a walk-in who says nothing about the card, someone who searches your clinic name later and calls a different line, a call taken on a line that is not tracked, or a referral from someone who saw the card. Treat tracking numbers as a directional signal about how a campaign is landing, not a complete or exact count of everything it produced.

Scope of the work

Our services are limited to what we agree for your campaign. If something is not in the agreed scope, it is not included.

Unless we agree it in writing for your campaign, a campaign does not include ongoing marketing management, digital advertising, social media, SEO, review management, event fees charged by third parties, email marketing, website design or changes, or handling patient calls, inquiries and scheduling. Responding to the people your campaign reaches is your team's job, and how well that is handled matters a great deal to how the campaign performs.
