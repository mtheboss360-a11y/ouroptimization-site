What carries over

The plan doesn’t change online.

Digital channels are planned after the market study and the community map, and they follow the same system as every other channel.

  1. MarketAds and listings point at the neighborhoods the research ranked.
  2. CommunityLocal search and social feeds sit on the same map as mailboxes, shops and community centers.
  3. ChannelsSearch and social go live alongside mail, flyers and events.
  4. OfferThe ad makes the same offer as the mailer, with the same headline.
  5. TrackingEach digital channel gets its own number, link or code.

How the map is built: Community Marketing. How responses are counted: Campaign Tracking.

Local search (SEO)

Show up when your market is searching.

On the community map

  • Local searchLocal SEO & Google AdsThe unpaid listings, then the paid ads

When someone nearby looks up a service you offer, the map results and local listings can shape who they call. Local SEO works on the parts of those results you control.

  • A complete, accurate Google Business Profile
  • Local pages for your core services and the area you serve
  • The same name, address and phone number on every listing
  • Reviews from real customers, with a reply to each

Google Ads

Paid search, held to your area.

The same touchpoint, local search, with paid placement. Ads are set to show only where you serve customers and only for searches that suggest someone wants to book.

  • Radius and ZIP targeting

    A distance around your location, or the ZIP codes the research ranked.

  • Search intent

    Keywords built on a service and a place, with job, training and do-it-yourself searches excluded.

  • Call and landing-page tracking

    A tracked number, and a landing page that carries the campaign’s offer code.

  • The languages people search in

    Ads and landing pages in another language where people in your market search in it.

Facebook & Instagram

Your offer, in the local feed.

On the community map

  • Social feedsFacebook & Instagram adsAimed at the area in your plan

Social ads reach your market between searches. They use the same creative as your print, so the ad and the mailer read as one business.

  • Targeted by location and, where the platform allows, by interests
  • Creative that matches your mailers and flyers
  • Promotion for the events and sponsorships in the plan
  • Clicks, bookings and offer-code redemptions reported

Promotional platforms

Groupon and deal sites, for a specific goal.

A deal site can introduce your business to people browsing for offers nearby. It is optional, and it has real costs: a deep discount, a share of each sale kept by the platform, and buyers who may not come back.

It is worth considering when a goal calls for it, such as filling new capacity or introducing a new service. When a campaign uses one, the offer is built to lead to a second visit, and redemptions are tracked with everything else.
