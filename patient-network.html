How the evening works

From the first call to the results sheet.

Four steps. You host the evening and make the offer. We handle the work around it.

  1. 01

    Plan

    We plan the evening with you: which dinner, the date, the restaurant, the menu set with the venue, and the offer you want to make. The price is fixed in writing before you commit.

  2. 02

    Invitations

    We design the invitations in your clinic’s name. Guests reply on an RSVP page in your clinic’s name, and we handle the reminder messages.

  3. 03

    The night

    We run the evening: name cards, seating, the food and the timing. You give a short talk and make your offer. Guests can book on the night, with an offer card that has its own code.

  4. 04

    The follow-up

    We handle the follow-up the next day. At 30 days you get a results sheet: who came, how many were new, and what they booked.
