Client Portal

In person, every month.

Deliveries, drop-offs and every stop tracked in your portal.

Deliveries

Warm coffee and fresh-baked treats.

At every visit, for the people working that shift. It is how a front desk gets to know our people, and your clinic, by name.

  • Warm coffee for the whole shift.
  • Fresh-baked breakfast treats, picked up the morning of the route.
  • A real introduction. When a business asks who we represent, we tell them: your clinic.

Drop-offs

Your materials, where people will see them.

With each business’s permission, we leave your printed materials at the counter and set up a personal code for every staff member we meet. Next month we restock them.

  • Your printed materials, placed where staff and customers will see them.
  • A personal tap-and-scan code for every staff member, so anyone they send shows up under their name.
  • Restocked every month, with new staff introduced as they join.

At every stop

The same three steps, every business on your route.

A route is 5 matched businesses near your clinic. You approve the list before we visit anyone.

  1. 01

    Bring treats and coffee

    Fresh-baked treats and warm coffee for the people who work there, the same for everyone, whether or not they ever send anyone.

  2. 02

    Meet the staff

    Introduce your clinic to them, using only wording you have approved, and leave your printed materials where staff and customers will see them, with the business’s permission.

  3. 03

    Set up the codes

    Each staff member we meet gets a personal tap-and-scan code, so anyone they send shows up under their name.
