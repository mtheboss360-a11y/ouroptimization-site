# ouroptimization.com

Our Wellness Network (a brand of OUR Optimization LLC) is a local marketing company in Chicago's western suburbs. Since October 22, 2026 the site leads with **the referral network for independent med spas**, at the owner's request: the network is the star of the site, not the dinners. The services read as three steps, in this order:

1. **Referral Network**: we map the businesses near the clinic (gyms and training studios, salons, cafés and restaurants, offices and small businesses), sign 5 to 10 of them on as partners and give each a partner kit. The home page headline: "Your neighbors bring you new patients. Your best patients bring the next ones."
2. **Flyer Network** (flyer drops): a representative visits the businesses the clinic chooses, on set field days, with a visit log after each one. Partners from the network ride the same route.
3. **Patient Network** (the patient dinners): the Plus-One Dinner (about 15 of the clinic's favourite patients each bring a friend who is not a patient yet) and the VIP Night (up to 30 of the clinic's best patients, an evening built around a new treatment or package). The owner gives a short talk and makes an offer they have approved; it is a sales evening and the site says so.

Then the extras: Online Search Network (Google Ads; the clinic pays Google directly, in its own account), Review Tags and Website Design, each a separate order.

**The name.** On October 22, 2026 the owner renamed the brand from Our Wellness Optimization to Our Wellness Network ("instead of Optimization, put Network"), and the services became networks: Referral Network, Flyer Network (was Flyer Drops), Patient Network (was Patient Dinners) and Online Search Network (was Google Ads); Review Tags and Website Design keep their names. The logo keeps the same mark and colors and only its last word changed (`assets/img/our-wellness-network-logo.png`, 480 x 174; the old logo file is deleted). The legal entity (OUR Optimization LLC), the domain (ouroptimization.com) and the email (mustafa@ouroptimization.com) do not change. In body copy the network name is used where a sentence names the service; the activity and the products keep their plain words ("flyer drops", "Track every drop", the Plus-One Dinner and the VIP Night, and Google Ads where it means Google's ad product). The favicon set is unchanged.

No price figure appears on the site (removed at the owner's request on October 21, 2026: a number up front scares people off). Each dinner gets one fixed quote, restaurant and food included, given after the 15-minute call and fixed in writing before the clinic commits, with no monthly contract. The referral and flyer networks have a flat monthly fee set in advance that never moves with referrals, month to month with 30 days' notice.

Since October 22, 2026 it is a multi-page site: every service has its own page and its own tab in the header (the owner asked for "everything its own tab ... every page with its own page").

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, no dependencies, no third-party requests.

## The client portal

`/client-portal` is a page about the portal, not the portal itself. The portal is a separate
application: a Node server with a SQLite database holding client accounts, sessions and campaign
records, built in `~/our-wellness-client-portal`. **It cannot run on Netlify**, which serves static
files and short-lived functions, so it needs its own host (Fly, Render, Railway, a small VPS) and
its own subdomain.

Until it is hosted, the page carries no sign-in link — there is nowhere to send a reader. When the
portal goes up, add the link to the page's two calls to action and to the header, and say so here.

The portal's sample data is a fictional clinic. The real ETHR LAB partner businesses are in the
`NAME_HASHES` guard in `tests/pages.test.mjs` and must never appear on this site.
