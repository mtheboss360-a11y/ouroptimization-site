# ouroptimization.com

Our Wellness Optimization (a brand of OUR Optimization LLC) is a local marketing company in Chicago's western suburbs. Since October 22, 2026 the site leads with **the referral network for independent med spas**, at the owner's request: the network is the star of the site, not the dinners. The services read as three steps, in this order:

1. **Referral Network**: we map the businesses near the clinic (gyms and training studios, salons, cafés and restaurants, offices and small businesses), sign 5 to 10 of them on as partners and give each a partner kit. The home page headline: "Your neighbors bring you new patients. Your best patients bring the next ones."
2. **Flyer Drops**: a representative visits the businesses the clinic chooses, on set field days, with a visit log after each one. Partners from the network ride the same route.
3. **Patient Dinners**: the Plus-One Dinner (about 15 of the clinic's favourite patients each bring a friend who is not a patient yet) and the VIP Night (up to 30 of the clinic's best patients, an evening built around a new treatment or package). The owner gives a short talk and makes an offer they have approved; it is a sales evening and the site says so.

Then the extras: Google Ads (the clinic pays Google directly, in its own account), Review Tags and Website Design, each a separate order.

No price figure appears on the site (removed at the owner's request on October 21, 2026: a number up front scares people off). Each dinner gets one fixed quote, restaurant and food included, given after the 15-minute call and fixed in writing before the clinic commits, with no monthly contract. Flyer drops and the referral network have a flat monthly fee set in advance that never moves with referrals, month to month with 30 days' notice.

Since October 22, 2026 it is a multi-page site: every service has its own page and its own tab in the header (the owner asked for "everything its own tab ... every page with its own page").

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, no dependencies, no third-party requests.

## Deploy

Netlify site linked to this repo, branch `main`. No build command; publish directory `.` (set in `netlify.toml`). Pull requests get a Netlify deploy preview. Show the owner a deploy preview before anything goes to production.

Forms use **Netlify Forms** (no function, no secrets):
- **Site configuration > Forms > Enable form detection** must be on. After turning it on the first time, redeploy.
- **`dinner-call`**: the "Book a 15-minute call" form on the contact page (`contact.html`, `/contact`, `#call`, script `assets/call.js`). It posts to `/thanks` (`thanks.html`, `noindex`) and has a `bot-field` honeypot. Fields: `name`, `clinic`, `phone`, `email` (optional), `town`, `best_time`, `dinner` and `message` (optional). The `dinner` field keeps its name (so Netlify keeps one column for old and new submissions) but its legend is "What would you like to talk about?" with, in this order: Referral network or flyer drops / Plus-One Dinner / VIP Night / Google Ads, review tags or a website / Not sure yet. (Submissions before October 22, 2026 may read "Flyer drops or referral network".) A `?about=` link does not preselect a radio; it only prefills the message. Every call to action on the site links to `/contact`, with `?about=` where it fits: `patient-dinners`, `referral-network`, `flyer-drops`, `google-ads`, `review-tags` and `website-design` each prefill the message. Old links (`/?about=review-tags#call`) are redirected to `/contact?about=review-tags`.
- **`expo-vendor`**: the **Baby & Kids Health Expo** practice sign-up page (`expo-vendors.html`, `/expo-vendors`, short link `/vendors`). Printed QR codes may point at it: do not change its URL or form name. The page is `noindex`; update the "Where" and "When" facts once the venue and date are set. Parent follow-up requests at the expo are collected outside this site (a Google Form and Sheet per practice, each with its own QR code).
- Email alerts: **Site configuration > Notifications > Form submission notifications > Add notification > Email notification**, one for each form, to mustafa@ouroptimization.com. Submissions are also listed under **Forms** in the Netlify UI.
- After changing form fields, redeploy so Netlify re-reads the form from the static HTML.
- The old `campaign-estimate` form (`estimate.html`) is retired. Any past submissions can still be viewed or deleted under Forms in the Netlify UI, and the privacy policy covers them.

## Content rules

- No prices or fee figures anywhere: not in copy, alt text, comments, tests or this file. Describe how a price is set (one fixed quote per dinner; a flat monthly fee set in advance for flyer drops and the referral network), never a number. A test scans every text file for dollar figures.
- Sound experienced through specifics: what we do, how often, what the clinic gets and what is in writing, in confident present tense (the owner asked for this on October 22, 2026). No self-deprecating or "we're new" lines: nothing about being set up, a first round, nothing having run yet, or having no results to show.
- Never invent results, numbers, client counts, years in business, reviews or testimonials to sound experienced. No fake reviews, testimonials, statistics, client logos or "as seen on". Add a result only with the clinic's written OK and figures that back it.
- No medical or results claims. Never promise a number of patients, leads, partners, bookings, a callback time or a return.
- Never name a prospect, or anyone from a prospect's or a partner's documents. Describe the agreement only as "our flyer drop agreement" or "a two-location practice" (Location A and Location B). The gyms in the ETHR LAB kit and the people behind it are never named. A test checks every text file against a list of hashed names, so the names are not spelled out in the repository either.

- No other business is named as a client except Grace Pediatrics (the existing client proof). The spa whose stand photo `/review-tags` showed until October 22, 2026 is no longer named or shown anywhere in the repository (a test checks every text file).
- Make no promises about patient data. It fails if any stylesheet, script, font or image on `/contact` or `/thanks` returns an error, and checks the `?about=` prefills, the `/?about=flyer-drops` redirect to `/contact?about=flyer-drops`, the old home anchors (`/#faq` opens `/faq`), `/estimate?package=...` and the `/#call?package=...` fallback in `site.js`. The local server must apply the `netlify.toml` redirects, including the query rule, the way Netlify does.
