# ouroptimization.com

Our Wellness Network (a brand of OUR Optimization LLC) is the organic community relationships team for independently owned med spas, starting in Chicago and the surrounding suburbs. Tagline: "Your Organic Community Relationships Team." Core message: "We put your brand into the community." Since October 23, 2026 the site presents one managed growth service, not separate businesses: local relationship development, on-the-ground brand representation, coordinated customer-acquisition campaigns, and growth tracking and reporting, all run from one growth plan. We generate the opportunities; the client's team handles inquiries, follow-up, booking and sales. The ways we execute, in this order:

1. **Referral Network**: we map the businesses near the clinic (gyms and training studios, salons, cafés and restaurants, offices and small businesses), sign 5 to 10 of them on as partners and give each a partner kit. The home page headline: "Your neighbors bring you new patients. Your best patients bring the next ones."
2. **Flyer Network** (flyer drops): a representative visits the businesses the clinic chooses, on set field days, with a visit log after each one. Partners from the network ride the same route.
3. **Patient Network** (the patient dinners): the Plus-One Dinner (about 15 of the clinic's favourite patients each bring a friend who is not a patient yet) and the VIP Night (up to 30 of the clinic's best patients, an evening built around a new treatment or package). The owner gives a short talk and makes an offer they have approved; it is a sales evening and the site says so.

Then the extras: Online Search Network (Google Ads; the clinic pays Google directly, in its own account), Review Tags and Website Design, each a separate order.

**The name.** On October 22, 2026 the owner renamed the brand from Our Wellness Optimization to Our Wellness Network ("instead of Optimization, put Network"), and the services became networks: Referral Network, Flyer Network (was Flyer Drops), Patient Network (was Patient Dinners) and Online Search Network (was Google Ads); Review Tags and Website Design keep their names. The logo keeps the same mark and colors and only its last word changed (`assets/img/our-wellness-network-logo.png`, 480 x 174; the old logo file is deleted). The legal entity (OUR Optimization LLC), the domain (ouroptimization.com) and the email (mustafa@ouroptimization.com) do not change. In body copy the network name is used where a sentence names the service; the activity and the products keep their plain words ("flyer drops", "Track every drop", the Plus-One Dinner and the VIP Night, and Google Ads where it means Google's ad product). The favicon set is unchanged. On October 23, 2026 the tagline became "Your Organic Community Relationships Team." with the core message "We put your brand into the community."; the legal entity (OUR Optimization LLC), the domain and the email stay.

**Prices.** On October 21, 2026 the owner took every price figure off the site (a number up front scares people off). On October 22, 2026 the owner asked for a pricing page (`/pricing`) and a sample contract (`/sample-contract`), built and held on their own branch until the owner says to publish them. Price figures appear on those two pages only (and in `tests/pricing.test.mjs`, which locks them); every other page describes how a price is set and may link to `/pricing`. The pricing page opens with a drumroll and no figure, then reveals the starter pack for the first month, the referral network's flat monthly fee from month two (five new tap-to-sign-up stands and one field day each month), the flyer network per location, the online search network plus the ad budget the clinic pays Google directly, and the patient network as one flat fee per event, quoted per venue. Each dinner's quote includes the restaurant and food and is fixed in writing before the clinic commits, with no monthly contract. Monthly fees never move with referrals, and monthly services run month to month with 30 days' notice.

Since October 22, 2026 it is a multi-page site: every service has its own page and its own tab in the header (the owner asked for "everything its own tab ... every page with its own page").

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, no dependencies, no third-party requests.
