In writing

The ground rules, in writing.

  • A one-page partner agreement

    Each partner signs one. You promote each other. Nobody is paid per referral or per customer.

  • Your customers stay yours

    Partners never see who signed up. Your team makes the calls. We never receive your customer or patient records.

  • Your words only

    We only use words you have approved. We make no medical or health claims.
