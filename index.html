The staff courtesy

The staff courtesy: the same for everyone.

Every staff member at a partner business, from the front desk to every trainer and stylist, gets the same courtesy from your clinic. For your trial the default is a complimentary consultation, which costs you only a few minutes and often turns into a booking itself. You can choose something else, like a complimentary add-on or a product sample. It’s given whether or not they ever send anyone, which keeps it a courtesy and not a payment for referrals.

Questions about the courtesy

Compliance

The compliance part, in writing.

  • A one-page partner agreement

    Every partner business signs a one-page agreement: mutual promotion, nobody is paid per referral or per patient, no patient records are shared.

  • No patient records, ever

    Partners never see who they sent. Your front desk calls and books, and we never handle patient information.

  • Your words only

    We never describe your treatments in words you haven’t approved, and we make no medical claims.
