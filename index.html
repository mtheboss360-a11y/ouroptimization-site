Questions

Who is this for?

Local businesses in Chicago’s western suburbs whose customers live nearby: med spas and wellness, dental, chiropractic and physical therapy, gyms and fitness studios, salons and barbershops, restaurants and cafes, home services, and auto shops.

Which businesses do you visit?

Businesses near you that share your customers and don’t compete with you. Each partner agrees to promote one business of your kind: yours.

What do you bring on a visit?

Your approved materials, coffee and treats, and a personal card for each staff member we meet. Treats are shared with the whole staff on every visit, whether or not anyone refers a customer. Each card has a tap tag and QR code, so you can see which partnerships are working.

Are partner businesses paid for referrals?

No. Each partner signs a one-page agreement to promote each other, and nobody is paid per referral or per customer. Partners never see who signed up. Your team makes the calls.

What about healthcare practices?

Partner programs for healthcare practices are reviewed for compliance with applicable state rules before launch. We never receive patient records, and we make no medical or health claims. If you’d like your own counsel to review the partner agreement, we’ll send it to them.
