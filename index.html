The staff courtesy

The staff courtesy: you set it and cap it.

For your trial the default courtesy is a complimentary consultation, which costs you only a few minutes and often turns into a booking itself. You can choose something else, like a complimentary add-on or a product sample, and you set a cap, for example up to 3 staff per business, so a route means at most 15 courtesies. It’s the same for every staff member and is given whether or not they ever send anyone, which keeps it a courtesy and not a payment for referrals.
