How the evening works

From the first call to the results sheet.

Four steps. You host the evening and make the offer. We handle the work around it.

  1. 01

    Plan

    We plan the evening with you: which dinner, the date, the restaurant, the menu set with the venue, and the offer you want to make. The price is fixed in writing before you commit.

  2. 02

    Invitations

    We design the invitations in your clinic’s name, and you send them from your own list. Guests reply on an RSVP page in your clinic’s name, and we handle the reminder messages.

  3. 03

    The night

    We run the evening: name cards, seating, the food and the timing. You give a short talk and make your offer. Guests can book on the night, with an offer card that has its own code.

  4. 04

    The follow-up

    We handle the follow-up the next day. At 30 days you get a results sheet: who came, how many were new, and what they booked.

FAQ

Good questions, plain answers.

Can’t I just book a restaurant myself?

You can. The room is the easy part. The work is everything around it: invitations in your clinic’s name, the RSVP page, reminder messages, name cards and seating, the run of the evening, an offer card with its own code, the next-day follow-up and a results sheet at 30 days. We handle all of it, so you can host.

Will people actually come on a weeknight?

We can’t promise a number, and we won’t. What we can tell you is how the evening is set up: the invitation comes from your clinic, the guests are patients who already trust you (at the Plus-One Dinner, each brings a friend they chose), guests reply on an RSVP page, and reminder messages go out before the night. We pick the date with you.

Is it a sales evening?

Yes. You give a short talk and make an offer you have approved, and guests can book on the night. We say so plainly, because that is the point of the evening.

Do you ever see my patient list?

No. You send the invitations from your own list. We never receive, see or keep your patient list. Guests reply on an RSVP page in your clinic’s name. We see the replies guests send there, because we use them for the reminder messages, name cards and seating.

My rep already sponsors events. Why use you?

Keep them. If your Allergan or Galderma rep sponsors events, we plan the dinner with them. What we add is the work around the evening: invitations in your clinic’s name, the RSVP page, reminder messages, seating, the run of the night, an offer card with its own code, the next-day follow-up and a results sheet at 30 days.

What does it cost?

It depends on the dinner, the number of guests and the restaurant. The restaurant and food are included. We quote it for your clinic after the 15-minute call, the price is fixed in writing before you commit, and there is no monthly contract.
