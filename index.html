Treats for Staff

Treats for Staff: hospitality, every visit.

On every visit, our field team brings coffee and treats for the entire staff of each partner business: the front desk, the trainers, the stylists, whoever is on shift. It is how relationships start, and why staff are glad to see us walk in. The treats are modest by design: coffee, pastries and snacks.

Questions about Treats for Staff

In writing

The ground rules, in writing.

  • A one-page partner agreement

    Every partner business signs a one-page agreement: mutual promotion, and nobody is paid per referral or per customer.

  • Your customers stay yours

    Partners never see who signed up. Your team calls and books. If you are a health or medical practice, we never receive patient records.

  • Your words only

    We never describe your services in words you haven’t approved, and we make no medical or health claims.
