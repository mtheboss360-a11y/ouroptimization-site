How we work

Plain terms, written down.

    Each dinner has one fixed quote. Monthly services have a flat monthly fee, set in advance, that never moves with referrals.

  • Your call, your words

    You choose and approve every business we visit and every word we use about your clinic.

    We never receive, see or keep your patient list.

    Monthly services run month to month, with 30 days’ notice to end them. Dinners have no monthly contract.
