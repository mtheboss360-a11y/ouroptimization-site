How it works

Your brand, out in the community.

Three steps, from the right match to a tracked booking.

  1. 01

    We match you.

    Our business matching software finds non-competing local businesses that share your customers. You approve the list before we start.

  2. 02

    We run your routes.

    A rep visits a route of 5 matched businesses and introduces your clinic to their staff with your staff offer, a free treatment or sample from your clinic.
