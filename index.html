What we do

Three steps. Each one builds on the last.

Start with the referral network. Add the flyer network and the patient network when you are ready. Each step has its own page.

    Referral Network

    Start here: the businesses near you keep your name at their front desk.

    We map 15 to 20 businesses near your clinic, aim to sign 5 to 10 as partners and give each one a partner kit, so every lead shows where it came from.

    • Referral cards with its own code
    • A counter stand and flyers, with an NFC tag and a QR code

How we work

Plain terms, written down.

  • Fees set in advance

    Monthly services have a flat monthly fee, set in advance, that never moves with referrals. Each dinner has one fixed quote.

  • Your call, your words

    You choose every business on your flyer drop list, and approve every word we use about your clinic.

  • Reports in writing

    A visit log within five business days of every field day, and a report on your referral network every month.

  • Month to month

    Monthly services run month to month, with 30 days’ notice to end them. Dinners have no monthly contract.
